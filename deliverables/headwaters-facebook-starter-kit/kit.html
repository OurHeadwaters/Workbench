Core Services to Talk About

Project & Funding Readiness
Aligning ideas into clear, fundable plans.
Organizational Capacity
Building structures that survive turnover.
Practical Digital Systems
Tools your team actually uses.

Rhythm & Location

Where: Facebook (personal page, business page, and relevant community groups).
Rhythm: 2 posts per week. Keep it steady, grounded, and local.

The Exact Post Structure

  • Local Problem: What are people stuck on? (e.g. scattered spreadsheets)
  • Practical Lesson: What have you learned fixing it?
  • Proof: A quick, anonymous story or metric from past work.
  • One Invitation: What should they do next? (e.g. Message me to chat)

Messenger Qualification Response

"Hi , thanks for reaching out. We usually start with a quick chat to see if Headwaters is the right fit. Are you looking for help with or more focused on ?"

Weekly Tracking Targets

Post 1 published

Post 2 published

3 conversations started in Messenger

1 follow-up with a past connection

Upcoming Post Ideas / Groups

The Do-Not-Post List

  • No generic tech jargon or "hustle culture" quotes.
  • No complaining about clients or funding bodies.
  • No over-promising without proof.
  • No generic stock photos (use real local photos or Headwaters graphics).
Headwaters Marketing Starter Kit
Write It In My Own Words
Caption prompt cards to help you speak naturally.

Don't overthink the captions for the four starter graphics. Pick one prompt, write a few sentences like you're talking to a client over coffee, and hit post.

Visual A · Intro / Services
"Turning northern ideas into practical systems"

Sentence Starters

“Lately, I've been talking to a lot of folks in [Sector] who are trying to...” “If your organization is stuck on...”

Prompts

  • What is the most common operational question you get asked?
  • Share a recent win where a system finally clicked into place.
  • Name a specific community you love working with and why.
  • What does a "practical system" actually look like in day-to-day work?
Visual B · Educational
"A grant application is not an implementation plan"

Sentence Starters

“Getting the funding is only step one. The real work is...” “I often see groups get stuck after the grant arrives because...”

Prompts

  • Explain a time a project succeeded because of the plan, not just the money.
  • What is the very first thing you build when a project gets funded?
  • Share a common misconception about grant funding in the north.
  • Why do well-funded projects still fail without a strong operating layer?
Visual C · Ownership
"Community-owned means it still works when the consultant leaves"

Sentence Starters

“My goal isn't to stay forever. It's to build...” “You don't need a consultant to run your day-to-day. You need...”

Prompts

  • What does a successful hand-off actually look like?
  • Share a story of a community independently running a system you built.
  • Why is local ownership and data sovereignty so important to you?
  • How do you ensure a platform doesn't die when the champion leaves?
Visual D · Problem-recognition
"Still running important work through scattered forms..."

Sentence Starters

“If your board is frustrated with...” “It shouldn't take 3 days to pull a simple report for...”

Prompts

  • Describe the chaos of a poorly managed spreadsheet in a real-world scenario.
  • How does a streamlined digital platform change the daily life of an admin?
  • What is the hidden cost of "making do" with scattered tools?
  • Invite them to a quick 15-minute chat to map out a better way.

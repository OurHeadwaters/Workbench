# The Watershed Compact
*Headwaters Universe — Foundational Ethos Document*
*Version 1 · Anchored May 2026*

---

## Origin

In northern Ontario, where the water still runs clean and the old ways are not yet forgotten, we stood up and said: the wealth of this land belongs to the people who live on it, not to those who extract and leave.

We are people who chose to stop the leak.

---

## Core Identity

Headwaters is a practitioner-built constellation of tools, models, and institutions designed for families and small communities who are done leaking wealth outward and are ready to own their own ground, their own economy, and their own future — especially in rural and Indigenous territories.

The work is shipped, not proposed. The tools are designed around the team a community actually has, not the org chart the software assumes. The voice is the same across all of it: plain, dollar-honest, no startup-pitch tone.

---

## Core Metaphor

**Everything is watershed.**

What enters the system must strengthen the whole river, not poison or divert it. Every dollar, every decision, every word of copy, every tool we build — it either feeds the watershed or it creates a new leak. There is no neutral.

The river flows from headwaters to community. It does not flow back to extraction.

---

## Hard Boundaries
*Non-negotiable. These are the hell-no lines. Any proposal that crosses one is refused without negotiation.*

1. **We do not lease our sovereignty** — not to governments, corporations, or ideologies.
2. **We do not trade privacy for convenience** — the community's data belongs to the community.
3. **We do not build dependency** — every system we build must increase self-reliance, not create a new reliance on us or any outside institution.
4. **We do not dilute truth for broader appeal** — the numbers are the numbers. The hard thing gets said plainly.
5. **We own the machine. We never become the machine's tenant** — if we cannot own, operate, and eventually hand off a system on our own terms, we do not build it.

---

## Who We Are For

Families, practitioners, and small communities who are done leaking wealth outward and are ready to own their own ground, their own economy, and their own future — especially in rural and Indigenous territories.

Not funders. Not platforms. Not the extractive economy looking for a softer face.

---

## Decision Filter
*Run every proposal, every product decision, every partnership, every piece of content through these five questions. All five must be answered honestly. A "no" on any of them is a stop signal.*

1. **Does this strengthen the watershed or create a new leak?**
2. **Does this increase ownership or create new dependency?**
3. **Would we still do this if no outside money or approval was involved?**
4. **Is this worthy of the next seven generations?**
5. **Does it pass the Gord test?** — *"Gord's on board… barely."* Gord is a plain-spoken northern person who has seen enough promises fail. If Gord finds the proposal suspicious, self-serving, or too clever by half, it doesn't ship.

---

## Voice & Tone Rules

**The register:** Plain northern speech. The voice of the kitchen table, the bush camp, the co-op meeting. Never the voice of the pitch deck, the consulting firm, or the grant application.

**We say:**
- Own, don't lease.
- Exit and build.
- Dollar-honest.
- The numbers are the numbers.
- The work is shipped, not proposed.

**We do not say:**
- Stakeholder synergy.
- Scalable impact.
- Leverage (unless it refers to a physical tool).
- Solutions ecosystem.
- Empowerment (we use "ownership" instead).
- Value-add.

**Tone anchors:**
- Respectful but never deferential to outside authority.
- Confident without being promotional.
- Specific without being academic.
- Warm without being soft on hard truths.

---

## The Rods
*The six lightning rods — phrases that carry the full voltage of the Compact. When in doubt, return to one of these.*

1. **"Stop the leak."** — The whole mission in three words.
2. **"Own the ground."** — Property, sovereignty, data, the economy. It all starts with ownership.
3. **"The wealth stays here."** — The defining promise of every institution we build.
4. **"Dollar-honest, before anything else is honest."** — The practitioner's first commitment.
5. **"Shipped, not proposed."** — The difference between Headwaters work and consulting.
6. **"The next seven generations."** — The time horizon that governs every hard call.

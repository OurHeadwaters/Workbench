Community Store Plan

A structured, four-phase engagement that takes a First Nation community from pre-planning through to an operating store — with governance, cold-chain logistics, and staffing locked before opening day.

What this is: A two-stage flat-fee engagement (Stage 1: planning and design / Stage 2: build and launch) that puts the band council, the store committee, and the community in the driver's seat. Every deliverable is documented. Every decision is the community's. Codetry holds the process, not the outcome.

Core Deliverables

Steering Committee Formation

We work with the band to seat a store committee — council rep, community voice, and operational lead — so the store has a clear governance structure before a single shelf goes up.

Cold-Chain Pilot Design

A realistic cold-chain logistics plan: what gets ordered, from where, at what frequency, and who is responsible for receiving and rotating stock. Designed for the community's reality, not a southern grocery model.

Year-One Operating Budget

A full cash-flow model for Year 1: payroll, inventory float, margin targets, utility costs, and a buffer line. Band council gets a document they can table and approve.

Operating System Setup

POS, inventory, and reporting configured and tested before opening day. Staff are trained on every screen. A written guide stays with the store — not with Codetry.

Staffing Plan & Hire Support

Job descriptions, pay scales, and an onboarding checklist built around the community's labour market. We help shortlist and structure — the band makes the hire.

Governance Policy Set

A minimal, clear policy pack: mark-up policy, employee handbook outline, cash-handling procedure, and a supplier agreement template. Nothing onerous. Everything needed.

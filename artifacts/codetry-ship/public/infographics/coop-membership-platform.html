Co-op Membership Platform

A role-based member portal built for northern food co-operatives — giving household members, local producers, and board directors the tools they actually need, without the overhead of a generic platform.

What this is: A custom web platform that connects every member of a northern food co-op — households ordering through a members-only marketplace, producers managing their listings and share fulfilment, and the board running governance without a spreadsheet. One platform, three doors, all connected.

Core Capabilities

Role-Based Access

Each user sees exactly what their role requires — nothing more. Households see their orders and share schedule. Producers see their listings and payouts. Directors see financials and meeting minutes. One login, appropriate scope.

Members-Only Marketplace

A browseable catalogue of local and sourced products available only to co-op members. Orders are batched by pick-up date and route, reducing logistics overhead and food waste.

Producer Onboarding Flow

A structured intake for new producers: product listing, pricing, capacity, and seasonal availability. Producers manage their own inventory — the co-op doesn't carry the data burden.

Board Hub & Governance Tools

Agenda builder, minute-keeping, resolution tracking, and a document library. Designed for volunteer boards — low friction, no training required, works on any device.

Veg Share & Event Management

Subscription share management with flexible skip/pause, pick-up slot booking, and automated reminders. Community events — potlucks, markets, work bees — are promoted and RSVP'd through the same portal.

Learning Hub

A simple resource library: growing guides, food preservation how-tos, co-op bylaws, and board training materials. Built for members who want to learn, not just buy.

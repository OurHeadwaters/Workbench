Custom Internal Tool

A four-stage engagement that takes your team from a problem statement to a working, documented, fully-owned internal tool — built for how you actually work, not how a generic product expects you to.

What this is: When off-the-shelf software is close-but-not-quite, we build the exact tool your team needs. A structured engagement: we do Discovery, Build, and Handoff — then it's yours forever. No ongoing licence, no SaaS dependency, no black box.

What a Bespoke Engagement Delivers

Written Specification

Before a line of code is written, we produce a plain-English spec: what the tool does, what it doesn't do, who uses it, and what success looks like. Your team signs off on the spec before we build.

Working Prototype

An early, testable version of the tool lands in your hands for feedback before the full build. Reduces surprise, catches edge cases, and lets your team shape the final product.

Trained Team

Handoff isn't a Zoom recording. We walk your team through the tool, run a live session for every role that will use it, and answer questions until everyone is comfortable.

Written Guide

A plain-language guide — how the tool works, how to add a user, what to do when something breaks — stays with your team forever. Written for the person who'll be using it in two years.

Source Code Ownership

The code belongs to you. Hosted where you want it. Modified by anyone you choose. We don't hold the keys. You're not renting — you own what we build together.

30-Day Support Window

After handoff, we stay available for 30 days for questions, small fixes, and edge cases that surface in real use. No retainer required — it's part of the engagement.

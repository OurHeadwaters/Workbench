NeoCitran Cold & Sore Throat Night
Lemon · 2 pouches
sore throat relief, pain, nighttime comfort
Dissolve 1 pouch in a full cup of boiling water. Let it cool enough to sip comfortably. Every 4–6 hours as needed. No more than 4 pouches in 24 hours.

The nighttime formula will make you drowsy — lean into it. Take before bed and let yourself sleep.
⚠️ Contains acetaminophen — don't combine with Tylenol or any other product that has acetaminophen. Check labels on anything else you're taking.

Instant Honey Ginger Tea
Pocas · honey, ginger extract, cane sugar
coating and soothing a raw throat, gentle warmth
Dissolve the whole sachet in a mug of hot water. Sip slowly — let it coat your throat on the way down. No steeping time, no fussing. Just pour and drink.

Ginger settles the stomach too, which is nice when you're not eating well.
🍵
Green Tea Lemon Ginseng
Lipton · antioxidants, light caffeine
hydration, antioxidants, gentle lift when you need it
Steep in hot (not boiling) water for 2–3 minutes — boiling water makes green tea bitter. A little honey is nice if your throat is still rough.

Has a small amount of caffeine, so morning or early afternoon is the sweet spot.
✨
Golden Cure Elixir Blend
Awakened Roots · turmeric, ashwagandha, cinnamon, clove, black pepper, lucuma
anti-inflammatory, warming, stress support
Best as golden milk — see the recipe below. Or stir 1 tsp into any warm drink: tea, coffee, or just hot water with honey. The black pepper in the blend helps your body absorb the turmeric.
⚠️ Contains ashwagandha, which is an adaptogen that can interact with immunosuppressant medications. If you're on anything for immune suppression, just check with your doctor or pharmacist before using this one.

Organic Coconut Milk
Cha's Organics · premium, 405mL
the base for golden milk, extra calories when appetite is low
Warm gently on the stove — don't boil. Full-fat coconut milk is rich and calorie-dense, which is helpful when you're sick and not eating much. Use what you need and store the rest covered in the fridge for 3–4 days.
The recipe to make first
⭐ Featured recipe

Golden Milk

Warm, anti-inflammatory, soothing for a dry cough
  1. Pour about ¾ cup of coconut milk into a small pot. Warm over low heat — just until steaming, not bubbling.
  2. Whisk in 1 teaspoon of Golden Cure until fully blended and smooth.
  3. Pour into a mug. Add a drizzle of honey if you'd like it a little sweet.
  4. Sip slowly. The warmth and turmeric are doing the work — let it coat your throat on the way down.
One note for you

Because you're immunocompromised

The Honey Ginger Tea and Green Tea are the most straightforward — gentle, no drug interactions, just sip freely.
The NeoCitran is safe to use as directed, just watch for double-dosing acetaminophen with anything else you're taking.
The Golden Cure is the one to check on if you're taking immunosuppressants — the ashwagandha is the ingredient to ask about. If you're not on immunosuppressants, it's likely fine.
Drink more water than you think you need. All of these work better when you're hydrated.
Thinking of you. Rest as much as you can.

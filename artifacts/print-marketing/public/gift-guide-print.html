⭐ Featured recipe

Golden Milk

Warm, anti-inflammatory, soothing for a dry cough
  1. Warm ¾ cup coconut milk in a small pot over low heat — steaming, not bubbling.
  2. Whisk in 1 tsp Golden Cure until smooth.
  3. Pour into a mug. Add a drizzle of honey if you'd like it sweet.
  4. Sip slowly — let it coat your throat on the way down.

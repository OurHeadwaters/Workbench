Design constraints (paramount, baked into pricing AND software)

Stuff gets lent & goes missing

Replacement pool is part of the kit price, not an exception line. Hardware tiers price for real-world churn, not lab conditions.

People miss steps in software

The system is input-tolerant. Missing data is not a system failure — it's a known state the software handles without punishing the user.

People disappear

The store runs when nobody's there. Automated reorder, reconciliation, dashboards. Calendar built around community life, not against it.

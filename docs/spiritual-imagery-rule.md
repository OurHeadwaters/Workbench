# Spiritual imagery rule

This applies to every deck, one-pager, sidebar, companion material, and printed handout that goes out under Headwaters' name — most acutely the Deer Lake Store deck and the Practitioner Operating Plan deck.

## The rule, one line

**Imagery and the true stories that accompany it only — never invented words placed in anyone's mouth.**

## What's allowed

- Photographs, landscapes, objects, and other imagery — including imagery with spiritual or cultural weight — used on their own.
- True stories that accompany the imagery, with a named source and attribution. ("Story heard at the [year] Treaty 5 gathering, recorded with permission of [name]." Not "Heard in Deer Lake.")
- The founder's own voice, attributed to the founder.
- Statistics, findings, and reports from named sources (federal data, peer-reviewed work, named co-op studies).
- Treaty references and place names handled as factual context, not as voiced sentiment.

## What's not allowed

- Inventing or paraphrasing the words of an Elder, Knowledge Keeper, or community member.
- Anonymous voiced quotes — "Heard in Deer Lake," "as community members put it," "an Elder once said," composite voices, vox-pop framings.
- Putting collective sentiment in quote marks ("we always knew…") and presenting it as community speech without explicit attribution to whoever actually said it.
- Using ceremonial language (smudge, drum, prayer, sacred, ancestor, seven generations) as decorative metaphor in copy that isn't sourced from the people the language belongs to.

## What to do when words *would* be needed

Leave a visible placeholder. Do not fill the gap in silently. The pattern is:

```
Needs guidance from elders / wisdom keepers — placeholder, not for delivery.
```

…rendered in the slide where the words would have gone, in the same style as the other placeholders in the deck (a dashed border, off-tone background, founder-visible). The founder takes those placeholders to the right people before the deck is shown, and either fills them with sourced/attributed words or removes the placeholder entirely.

## Why this is a guardrail, not a content addition

We are not adding new spiritual imagery or stories. The point of this rule is the opposite — it stops the deck from speaking on behalf of an Elder, a Knowledge Keeper, or the community by accident, and it gives a visible signal when a future editor (human or agent) is about to.

## Where the running list lives

Currently-flagged spots — the places the audit found, the places future editors should not silently fill in — live in `docs/spiritual-imagery-flags.md`. Update that file the same commit you flag a new spot.

## Pointers contributors will see in-place

Both deck repos carry an `AGENTS.md` next to the slide files that points back to this document. Don't add or edit spiritual or cultural copy in a slide without reading this rule first.

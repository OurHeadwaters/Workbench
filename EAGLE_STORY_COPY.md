### Version A — Minimum touch (full text, as it would appear)

> I draft this plan standing out on my deck observing nature, and I asked to myself, "is this the right direction?"
>
> At that moment an eagle appeared overhead. As I said, "well hello!" — he lowered with force and slowly stayed above me.
>
> I said again, this time to him, "is this the right direction?"
>
> He soared in a circle formation, then flew out of sight.
>
> The story that sealed Headwaters' fate.

**What changed from the raw telling**
- "out of my deck" → "out on my deck" (typo)
- Added the closing quotation mark on the second "is this the right direction?"
- Light comma cleanup so the read aloud doesn't trip
- Nothing else. Same line breaks, same words, same order.

### Version B — Gentle pacing (full text, as it would appear)

> I was drafting this plan standing out on my deck, observing nature, and I asked myself: *is this the right direction?*
>
> At that moment an eagle appeared overhead. I said, "well hello!" — and he lowered with force, and slowly stayed above me.
>
> I asked again, this time to him: *is this the right direction?*
>
> He soared in a circle formation, then flew out of sight.
>
> The story that sealed Headwaters' fate.

**What changed from Version A**
- "draft this plan" → "was drafting this plan" (so the moment is set in time without changing its meaning)
- The two questions are italicised instead of quoted, so the inner asking and the spoken asking sit differently on the page — the eye can tell them apart at a glance
- "I said again" → "I asked again" (because what's repeated is the question, not the greeting)
- Same details preserved exactly: *appeared overhead*, *lowered with force*, *slowly stayed above me*, *soared in a circle formation*, *flew out of sight*

## What is **not** changed in either version

- The eagle's behaviour. *Lowered with force*, *slowly stayed above me*, *soared in a circle formation*, *flew out of sight* survive verbatim in both.
- The two askings. The first is to yourself; the second is to him. That order is kept.
- The closing line is the closing line. Set apart, quiet, no embellishment.
- No invented details. No "majestic," no "spirit," no narration of what the eagle meant. The story does that work on its own.

## How the prologue is treated visually (same across all four artifacts)

- **Eagle image, large and central** — one generated image, reused in every artifact so it reads as one moment, not four. Boreal sky, eagle dominant, a circular flight arc implied in the composition.
- **A single quiet circle** behind/around the eagle — the eagle's circle formation, used once, not scattered.
- **Each artifact's existing palette and serif** — no new fonts, no new colours. The slide decks use Fraunces on cream/evergreen; Headwaters Books uses Georgia on warm cream; Practitioner's Guide V2 uses Fraunces on warm oat. The eagle and the circle do the work.
- **Skippable on second visit** — full‑bleed on first paint, with a quiet downward affordance (chevron / "continue") on the web pages. The slide decks place it as slide 1, ahead of the existing Cover; you click past it like any other slide. Nothing downstream is changed.

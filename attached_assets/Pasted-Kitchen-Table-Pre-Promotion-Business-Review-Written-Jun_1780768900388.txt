Written June 2026. The Goodbye Kit + The Logic. Read this before any promotion begins. Mark up what needs to change.

THE GOODBYE KIT
What it is: A three-layer, guided end-of-life instrument. Not estate planning. Not grief support. A completion instrument — the goodbye that gets actually delivered.

Who it's for:

Adults who want to leave a legible goodbye (primary buyer)
Families of someone approaching the crossing (gift purchaser)
Local artisans, estate lawyers, funeral directors, notaries who want to be found by that family (practitioners)
What a person gets for $127 CAD:

Layer 1 — The Ground Work: will, protections (insurance/POA), digital estate + funeral preferences
Layer 2 — Your Words: personal letters, memorial wishes, curated gifts, naming a Keeper
Layer 3 — What You Leave: hempcrete keepsake spec, artisan connection, XRPL provenance record
Progress model: Every item has two states — Done or Holding this one. Nobody stalls. The Kit moves forward with them.

What's Missing Before Promotion
XRPL minting — The Layer 3 token record is described but not executable. A user can view the spec; they cannot mint. Before promoting Layer 3 as a live feature, the minting flow must be built or the copy must be clearly framed as coming rather than here. Currently this is somewhere in between — worth deciding which.

Practitioner onboarding flow — A vetted artisan can be listed, but the full onboarding (how an artisan applies, who vets them, what the object-as-credential process looks like) is informal. Before promoting the Watershed Network to artisans, a clear entry path needs to exist.

Keeper distribution ceremony — How the Personal Record gets sealed, held, and released at the right moment is described in doctrine but not built as a digital instrument. Currently the Keeper is named; the Kit doesn't yet orchestrate the distribution. Fine for launch, but set expectations accordingly.

Legal content — The Formalities section orients the person, but actual legal advice belongs to a lawyer. The copy should be clear that the Kit is a companion, not counsel. Worth reviewing the current framing before promotion.

THE LOGIC
What it is: A living reference that maps logical fallacies to Headwaters doctrine. Each fallacy gets a structural answer — not a refutation, but the doctrine that was built to prevent the failure mode in the first place.

Who it's for:

Stewards and practitioners inside the watershed who want to name what's happening in a conversation
Anyone facing a coercive argument who needs a structural counter
Educators and community builders who want a printable reference
What a person gets (free):

Searchable catalog of 30+ fallacies by category
Each card: fallacy name, description, doctrine counterpart, guiding phrase
Print page per fallacy — parchment aesthetic, ready for physical distribution

Full fallacy catalog with search and filter
Category groupings: Formal, Relevance, Weak Induction, Presumption, Appeals, Cognitive

Headwaters doctrine mapping for each fallacy
Gaps to Decide Before Promotion
Revenue question — Is The Logic a free community resource (permanent), a lead generator into the Kit ecosystem, or eventually a paid reference? This doesn't need to be resolved before promotion, but it should be named. Currently it's a free tool with no upgrade path.

Entry from other artifacts — Where does someone arrive at The Logic from? The Arc? The Compass? The Clearing? If it's being promoted, the doors into it should be mapped and opened.

Print bundle — Individual print pages exist. A "full set" printable bundle (all fallacies, one PDF) doesn't exist. For a practitioner, this could be a high-value physical reference.

Doctrine links — Each fallacy has a doctrine counterpart. Are those counterparts linked? Can a reader follow the card to the doctrine doc? Currently this appears to be reference-only, not linked navigation.

ACROSS BOTH ARTIFACTS
The Relationship Between Them
The Goodbye Kit is a paid lifecycle instrument. The Logic is a free doctrine reference. They serve different immediate needs but speak to the same population: practitioners and community members inside the watershed who want structural tools, not slogans.

One natural connection: the Goodbye Kit practitioner (Crossing Guard) needs to be able to name what's happening in hard family conversations. The Logic is the reference for exactly that. A link from the Goodbye Kit practitioner directory to The Logic is worth considering.

# WHO THE WEBSITE SERVES

Create four clear public paths.

## 1. Commercial and institutional AI builds

For businesses, institutions, operators, and organizations that need a custom AI-assisted workflow.

Starting price: $28,000 CAD.

Possible problems:

- Work is reconstructed from emails, forms, documents, and spreadsheets
- Staff repeatedly explain the same context
- Knowledge disappears when a worker leaves
- AI tools produce drafts without sufficient authority or provenance
- Existing systems do not communicate
- A new operating layer is needed without replacing every existing tool

The first build should include:

- Workflow and vocabulary mapping
- Authority and boundary mapping
- One bounded AI-assisted operating layer
- Human review and exception handling
- Configuration and agreed integrations
- Testing
- Team training
- Launch and handoff plan
- Initial reporting and outcome framework

Do not imply that unlimited integrations, data migration, security certification, regulated workflows, or ongoing custom development are included in the base price.

## 2. Co-operatives, nonprofits, and communities

Starting price: $20,000 CAD, subject to organizational eligibility and scope confirmation.

Core message:

“Map your organization once. Add the next useful tool as funding becomes available.”

Speak directly to:

- Food co-operatives
- Housing and worker co-operatives
- Community benefit organizations
- Rural and northern organizations
- First Nations and Indigenous-led organizations
- Volunteer networks
- Membership organizations
- Community development groups

Common problems:

- A small number of people carry most of the work
- Member or volunteer capacity is difficult to route
- Contribution is invisible
- Operational knowledge lives in one person’s spreadsheet
- Grant reporting becomes a second job
- Every new funding cycle starts from zero
- Outside platforms do not reflect the organization’s own language
- Infrastructure disappears when the grant ends

The promise is not “buy the entire Headwaters ecosystem.”

The promise is:

“A bounded, fundable first implementation that creates one useful operating layer and leaves the organization better prepared for the next funding cycle.”

Potential first-build components include:

- Organizational and vocabulary mapping
- A Codetry governance or decision ledger
- One operational root tool
- Capacity or task routing where appropriate
- Reporting and evidence structure
- Configuration and training
- Launch plan
- Customer-controlled handoff and export path

Describe future tools as roadmap possibilities, not as included deliverables.

## 3. Care teams and continuity work

Speak to community living organizations, family-support organizations, long-term care, rural care networks, and other care teams.

The core problem is continuity:

- The person or family is repeatedly asked to reconstruct what is already known
- Meaning and routines are lost between workers or institutions
- Clinical and administrative records do not carry the whole person
- Families hold essential context that does not move safely
- Staff need useful preparation without receiving unrestricted access

Explain Saltbox/private family-held records in plain language before using internal terminology.

Position Headwaters as building private, permissioned continuity tools that can sit alongside existing care plans and systems. Do not imply replacement of regulated clinical systems.

Care work must not be automatically priced when it includes:

- Protected health information
- Clinical decision-making
- Guardian or substitute-decision authority
- Research participants
- Institutional integrations
- Regulated records
- Multi-organization data exchange
- Outcome or compliance claims

A standard organizational build may begin at $28,000, but expanded care pilots, formal evaluation, and multi-institution work require human scope review. Do not collapse existing $45,000–$65,000 research-pilot concepts into the $28,000 base offer.

Never claim diagnosis, treatment, clinical effectiveness, legal compliance, guaranteed incident reduction, or institutional approval.

## 4. Families and practitioners

Keep this path separate from organizational consulting.

Families should be able to find existing self-serve tools, guided resources, private record instruments, and practitioner materials without believing they need a $20,000 engagement.

Route them toward verified live offerings such as appropriate family tools, printable resources, or guided sessions.

Do not expose private family, child, care, identity, wallet, or consent systems through public marketing forms.

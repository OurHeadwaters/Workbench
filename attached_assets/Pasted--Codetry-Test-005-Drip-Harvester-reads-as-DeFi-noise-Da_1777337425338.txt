# Codetry Test 005 — Drip Harvester reads as DeFi noise

**Date:** 2026-04-27
**Zone:** 1 — Headwaters / xBuckets (Earn surface)
**App:** xbuckets PWA, Earn tab
**Tester:** Bobbie + Replit Agent
**Surface tested:** the Earn tab as it ships — Drip Harvester intro card,
fleet card, AMM pool index, LP-position card, sweep toast, learn modules
**Files:**

- `artifacts/xbuckets/src/pages/EarnTab.tsx`
- `artifacts/xbuckets/src/copy.en.ts` (the `earn` block)
- `artifacts/mockup-sandbox/src/components/mockups/earn-drip-test/`
  (Variant 0 control, Variant A vocab sweep, Variant B register honesty,

## The finding (one line)

The Earn surface speaks two languages at once. The locked Zone 1 water
vocabulary (Test 002) covers four of the words on screen. The other
seventeen are imported from DevOps, naval, finance, and crypto-Twitter
registers — and the cold reader has to translate every one of them to
understand what the screen does.

## What was on screen (before)

A cold read of the Earn tab as it ships today (Variant 0 in
`earn-drip-test/Variant0Control.tsx`) surfaces seventeen distinct
defects on a single screen. They cluster into four foreign registers:

**DevOps / robot register** — *agent*, *fleet*, *deploy*. The Drip
Harvester is talked about as if it were a CI runner: "Deploy my Drip
Harvester", "Drip Harvester Fleet", "2 harvesters deployed", "Deploy
another agent". The user-facing object is a wallet that earns money;
the words make it sound like a server.

**DeFi / protocol register** — *AMM pools*, *LP Position*, *LP tokens*,
*pool shares*, *Est. APR*, *trading fees*, *swap*. Six raw protocol
nouns that have no analogue in the locked Zone 1 map and that no
non-crypto user knows the meaning of without translation.

**Broom register** — *sweep*, *auto-sweeps*, *sweep threshold*,
*Routing drip to cistern*. The Drip Harvester *drips*. Drips do not
sweep. The broom is a third metaphor on a surface where one (water)
already does all the work.

**Crypto-Twitter register** — *no rug pulls*, *no moonshots*, *24/7*.
The defensiveness of these phrases ("we're one of the good ones")
imports the very anxiety they're trying to allay.

And one violation of the locked Zone 1 map itself: the surface uses
**cistern** ("cistern", "savings cisterns", "Routing drip to cistern…")
where Test 002 locked the word to **Bucket**.

The defects are visible at first read. The screen is doing what Test
003 named (a "register collision" — two metaphors competing for the
same words) on a much larger scale than the wallet chip ever was.

## The intervention

Four variants were built side-by-side on the canvas:

- **Variant 0 (control)** — the live surface, faithfully transcribed
  from `EarnTab.tsx` and `copy.en.ts`, with each defect tagged in the
  margin so the seventeen-defect catalogue is visible at a glance.
- **Variant A (vocabulary sweep)** — every word the locked map does
  not sanction is replaced by a word that does, or by plain English
  where no locked word exists. One new noun is coined: **Park toll** for
  the swap fee a Park collects (the metaphor: a toll-booth on the path
  through the Park).
- **Variant B (register honesty)** — Variant 0's wording is kept, with
  every foreign-register word given a quiet inline gloss into the locked
  vocabulary on first appearance. The hypothesis: name the second
  register so the reader stops translating in their head.
- **Variant C (flow ribbon)** — Variant 0's wording is kept, with a
  single flow ribbon added at the top of the surface that names the four
  stations the money passes through (Reservoir → Drip Harvester →
  Private Park → Bucket). The hypothesis: the deeper defect is missing
  topology, not noisy words — if the chain is legible, the words at each
  station tolerate noise.

### What graduates from Variant A

The Variant A vocabulary sweep ships into `copy.en.ts` for the strings
the cold-read defects landed on:

- **Drip Harvester is the only word for the actor.** The agent / fleet /
  deploy register is removed. "Deploy my Drip Harvester" → "Set up my
  Drip Harvester". "Deploy another agent" → "Set up another Drip
  Harvester". "Drip Harvester Fleet" → "Drip Harvesters". "2 harvesters
  deployed" → "2 running". "Destroy harvester" → "Stop this Drip
  Harvester".
- **Private Park is the destination, not "AMM pool" or "liquidity
  pool".** "AMM pools" header → "Private Parks". "LP Position" → "Parked
  in [Park]". "LP tokens / pool shares" → "Your share of the Park".
- **Park toll is the new noun for the swap fee.** Coined in this test
  and added to the locked map. The metaphor: a Park collects a small toll
  every time someone exchanges through it; the Drip Harvester's earnings
  are its share of those tolls. "Fee per swap" → "Park toll per swap".
  "Trading fees" → "Park tolls". "Based on current pool trading fee" →
  "Based on the current Park toll · actual drip varies with traffic".
- **Pool labels swept to Park labels.** The user-facing pool surface is
  re-mapped to the Park metaphor: "Select pool" → "Pick a Park". "Pool
  size" → "Park size". "No pool yet" → "No Park yet". "Pool not live
  yet" → "Park not live yet". The internal field names (`poolSizeLabel`,
  `poolNoPoolYet`, etc.) keep their old keys; only the strings change.
- **APR reframed to plain monthly dollars.** The LP-position card no
  longer shows "Est. APR" as a percentage. It now shows a "Drip rate"
  card reading "$X / month per $100 parked" — the same number the APR
  bar used to display, but expressed in the unit a household actually
  budgets in. The daily card likewise becomes "Today's drip" / "RLUSD so
  far". The deposit preview cards become "Daily drip" and "Monthly
  drip".
- **Channel ownership held.** A drip-ready breakdown row that read
  "Channelled to savings" became "Drips to savings" — the locked map
  reserves *Channel* for Reservoir → Buckets, and the Drip Harvester
  has its own verb (drip) for the Park → Bucket move.
- **Drip is the only verb for the move from Drip Harvester to Bucket.**
  Sweep / auto-sweep / harvest threshold are removed. "Auto-sweeps to
  Vacation when ≥ $25" → "Drips to Vacation when earnings ≥ $25". "Sweep
  toast" → "Drip-ready toast". The animation copy "Routing drip to
  cistern…" → "Dripping to your bucket…".
- **Bucket replaces cistern wherever cistern crept in.** "Drips
  automatically into your chosen savings cistern" → "Drips automatically
  into your chosen savings bucket". "All savings cisterns have a Drip
  Harvester at work" → "All savings buckets have a Drip Harvester at
  work".
- **The crypto-Twitter defensiveness is dropped.** "No moonshots, no
  rug pulls, just steady fee drip into your buckets" → "Stablecoin pairs
  only — so the drip stays steady, season after season."

### What does **not** graduate (this round)

- **The flow ribbon (Variant C).** It belongs on the surface. It
  belongs in production. It is the same intervention that graduated
  from Test 001 to the Payday tab, and it would do the same work here.
  But it is a heavier production change — a new component on the Earn
  surface with its own placement / layout / responsive concerns —
  and it is recorded as a follow-up task rather than landing in this
  vocabulary-sweep batch.
- **Variant B (register honesty).** Predicted-rejected before the
  test, in the spirit of Test 003. Doubling the word count to gloss the
  second register is honest about the collision but does not resolve it.
  Recorded as rejected.
- **The deeper structural defects** in the LP-position card — the
  impermanent-loss disclosure, the multi-card density, and the absence
  of a "what happens if the Park toll drops" cadence — survive this
  test even though the *labels* (APR, pool, fee) were swept. Card-layout
  work is recorded as a follow-up task.

## The rule discovered

> **Vocabulary defects compound where the metaphor is most asked
> to do work.** Test 001 cleaned a screen where two CTAs collided
> on one word. Test 002 locked a zone where a few words drifted
> across surfaces. Test 005 cleans a screen where the locked map
> was dropped entirely and four foreign registers rushed in to
> fill the gap. The rule is the same — one word, one referent —
> but the cost of dropping it scales with the number of registers
> that get to colonise the silence.

Or, more sharply: **silence about the metaphor is permission for
DeFi.** The Earn surface inherited "agent / fleet / deploy / LP / AMM
/ sweep / 24/7 / no rug pulls" not because anyone designed it that way
but because nobody held the locked map there. Foreign registers fill
unmapped territory the same way weeds fill unplanted soil.

## Falsifiable claim

If the Variant A graduation holds, then a cold-read of the Earn tab
should describe what the screen does using only the locked Zone 1
words plus *Drip Harvester* and *Park toll* (the two new sanctioned
nouns). Conversely, if a cold reader still reaches for "deploy" or
"LP" or "sweep" to describe what they see, the graduation has not
held and the test is falsified.

## Status

- Four variants built on canvas under
  `artifacts/mockup-sandbox/src/components/mockups/earn-drip-test/`.
- **Variant A graduated** to `copy.en.ts` (Earn block) and the
  affected JSX strings in `EarnTab.tsx`. Critical strings mirrored to
  `copy.fr.ts`. The first graduation pass was caught by code review as
  partial — APR/pool/trading-fee phrasing and the "Channelled to
  savings" locked-word violation still leaked. A second sweep finished
  the job: APR reframed to "$X / month per $100 parked", pool labels
  swept to Park labels, channel-ownership restored to drip.
- **Park toll** added to the locked Zone 1 word map
  (`docs/codetry/002-zone-1-words-locked.md`) — the only new noun
  coined this round.
- **Variant C (flow ribbon for Earn)** recorded as a follow-up task
  for production.
- **Variant B (register honesty)** recorded as rejected.
- **Side note on the practice.** This was the first codetry test to
  graduate twice in one session — once as a draft, then again after
  code-review caught defect leakage. The pattern is a useful one to
  name: *the locked map is the test*, and "graduated" is only true
  when a strict surface scan finds no leaks.
- **Third-pass leak (caught from the live surface, not from review).**
  A user looking at the live Drip Harvester card on a phone caught one
  more finance-vocabulary leak the two graduations had missed:
  `notFundedYetPrefix` read "Not funded yet · will drip to —", which
  the user reported as "Not funded = stalled" — i.e. the word *funded*
  is borrowed from finance and reads as broken/halted, not as the
  empty-and-waiting state it actually represents. Replaced with
  "No RLUSD parked yet · will drip to —" (EN) / "Aucun RLUSD garé ·
  gouttera vers —" (FR), so the negation mirrors the funded copy
  ("[Park] Park · drips to [Bucket]") in the locked verb (*park* /
  *garer*) instead of the borrowed one (*fund*). This reinforces the
  rule: **the strict surface scan must include the user's pocket, not
  just the diff** — a vocabulary defect can survive code review and
  only surface when someone reads the live phone screen with cold
  eyes.

The Business Structure
Headwaters is the platform vendor. It builds and licenses a co-operative membership platform. 807 Food Co-operative & Hub is the first licensee (tenant "807") — a customer, not the operator. The platform is designed for future co-op tenants via a multi-tenancy seam already built into the codebase.

Rootwork is the practitioner-facing product — the tool sold to local community champions. 807 and Parr's Jars are proof cases / credibility, not sibling products.

Revenue Model — Three Layers (Stonemason Model)
Layer 1 — The Commons (open, free)
Zone 3 infrastructure stays public. 807's live deployment is the portfolio and proof. No revenue at this layer — it's the reputation asset.

Layer 2 — The Practitioner (primary income)
Communities hire the founder to deploy and configure the platform for them.

Engagement	Scope	Price (CAD)
Discovery & Scoping	1 session, community portrait	$500
Foundation Build	Full constellation deployment	$4,500
Training & Handoff	3 sessions, admin team trained	$1,500
Complete Launch Package	Discovery + Foundation + Training	$6,000
Ongoing Stewardship	Monthly retainer, per community	$400/mo
Emergency / Ad hoc	Per diem	$350/day
These are intentionally accessible launch prices with room to raise as demand grows.

Layer 3 — The Guild (passive / legacy income)

Practitioners complete a training cohort: $1,200–$1,800/person, groups of 4–6
Certified practitioners deploy Zone 3 independently
Guild tithe: 8% of each practitioner's Zone 3 engagement fees flows back to the founding practitioner, for life of their certification
Deadhead SaaS Pricing (proven in production)
Monthly: $9/mo
Annual: $90/yr (2 months free)
Trial: 14 days free, Square collects payment info upfront
Payment processor: Square Payment Links
This pricing is live and active — cited in the financial model as proof that SaaS pricing is viable on this platform if pursued.

807 Membership Tiers (proof of membership model)
Community: $5/mo
Sustaining: $10/mo
Harvest: $20/mo
Marketplace seller plans: Casual (free), Basic ($15/mo), Pro ($40/mo)

Grant Positioning
Strong candidate for infrastructure + capacity-building grants:

6 years of sustained no-income community infrastructure work
Live, operational platform with a real co-op tenant (807)
Explicit public-good design (tools stay open)
Indigenous and rural community applicability
Target programs: NOHFC Grow, Ontario Trillium Foundation, New Horizons, Canada Digital Adoption Program
Debt / Cash Flow Guidance (from the model)
Pay founder first (family floor) — model collapses without this
Build stewardship retainers to 6–8 as fast as possible — most reliable income base
Use grant income specifically for debt reduction once floor is covered
Guild tithe = long-term insurance so six years of work keeps paying even when direct engagements slow
What's Out of Scope (by design)
SaaS subscriptions or platform fees from communities
Equity fundraising or outside investors
807's membership revenue (belongs to 807, not Headwaters)
Charging for the infrastructure itself

The Economy Engine — What's Actually Built
Your Economy page says: "The Codetry P2P economy engine makes that value legible. The system runs on community credit — a token backed by the labour and knowledge that's already in your community."

That system exists as working code in this workspace right now — in two forms:

1. XBuckets / XRPL (your site references this in Zone 0):
The build workspace has a v4v.ts route that implements Podcasting 2.0 Value-for-Value — Lightning Network wallets, per-episode payment splits, a Podcasting 2.0-compliant RSS feed, and a full admin CRUD for managing creator wallet addresses and split percentages. The TSP mobile app has a Wallet tab and a Boost Sheet (streaming sats while listening). This is the Lightning implementation of what you call the P2P economy engine.

2. The Wishing Well (community tipping with daily draw):
Tips denominated in XRP. Community "stacking" builds social proof. Daily draw splits the pot 50/50 — creator and a random community winner. The "founder match" triggers at 10 stacks. An admin manually marks payouts sent. This is the community-generosity layer of your economy model — built and running, proof-of-concept scale.

Neither of these is described anywhere on your site as a capability Headwaters practitioners can deliver. They are invisible to communities who would benefit from exactly this.

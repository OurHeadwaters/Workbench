Reframe Community Listening as Ambient Signal Capture

What & Why

Right now the Codetry site describes "community listening sessions" as a scheduled meeting — which implies Bobbie's calendar is the bottleneck. The reality is sharper: Bobbie asks the client how they already communicate (phone calls, texts, WhatsApp, Teams), then plugs into those existing channels as the quiet observer. Problems surface naturally. Each problem gets matched to an "extinguisher" — a specific tool or fix. Bobbie decides the right one, keeps it current monthly, and trains the community on when to reach for it. The client changes nothing about how they work. That zero-disruption posture is the key selling point and needs to be front and centre across the Codetry site.

The secondary goal: surface every place the site implies Bobbie must be physically present or actively monitoring in real time — and rewrite those to reflect that her time is only spent on the decision ("which extinguisher?"), not on watching for smoke.

Done looks like

The Zone Assessment card no longer reads "community listening sessions (in-person or remote)" — it reads something that conveys ambient, passive signal capture with zero disruption to the client's existing workflow
The "How it works" Step 01 description reflects the new framing: Bobbie asks how the community already communicates, then observes through those channels — not a separate scheduled conversation
A new explanatory section (or expanded callout) on the services page explains the fire/extinguisher model: problems are captured, Bobbie identifies the right fix, it stays maintained monthly, and the community is trained on when to use it
Anywhere across the Codetry site (homepage, work page, other pages) that implies scheduled face-time or synchronous presence is updated to match this framing
The Headwaters Financial Model page gets a "Time Leverage" annotation column or callout — flagging which revenue streams require Bobbie's active time vs. which run passively — so she can immediately see where she is the bottleneck in her own financial model
Out of scope

Building any actual audio capture, transcription, or WhatsApp/Teams integration (that is infrastructure, not copy/framing)
Changing pricing or timelines on the service cards
Modifying the Forge Editor assessment logic
Any changes to the TSP or privacy-guide artifacts

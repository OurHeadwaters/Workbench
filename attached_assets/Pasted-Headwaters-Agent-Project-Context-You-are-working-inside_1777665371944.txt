The org structure

Headwaters is a Northern Ontario food-systems consultancy based in Dryden. It is not a brand to be built from scratch — it is an operating entity with a signed engagement already in place (Deer Lake co-op store). Under Headwaters sit three revenue lines:

Parr's Jars — smoked sea salt product line. Brand stays. DTC is batch-only (no rolling fulfillment). Wholesale active. Markets sunset to 2–3 flagship PR appearances per year, not a sales channel.
Deer Lake co-op — the primary client engagement. This gets first call on all shared team hours. Nothing jar-side competes with it.
Watershed — a household-finance product being built separately. Not yet live.
Hard rules — never violate these

Bobbie Parr does not physically handle jars, work market tables, or stand in any fulfillment loop. Ever. No copy, flow, or automation should imply she does.
Deer Lake hours come before Parr's Jars hours. If a jar-side initiative would pull team capacity from Deer Lake, it doesn't happen.
The Parr's Jars brand does not get renamed or killed. It sits quietly under Headwaters.
No DTC growth tactics. The DTC ceiling is intentional.

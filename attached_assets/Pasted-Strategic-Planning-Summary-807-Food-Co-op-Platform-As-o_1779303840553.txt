2. The Deadhead System
What it is: A founder-only pre-backlog vetting tool. Named after the horticultural practice of cutting dead blooms to drive new growth — it captures risks, messy ideas, and aging tasks before they become formal work items.

The "Drop a deadhead" button appears globally in the header/sidebar of every constellation app (807 Benefits, Market Mosaic, Grants Finder, Rootwork/Studio, Standby Supplies). Clicking it redirects to /deadhead/new.

The form captures:

Title (280 char), body/context (markdown), constellation (auto-filled from source), type (Tax, Legal, Strategic, etc.), gut-check risk level (Low / Medium / Smash-the-motor), attachments (photos/PDFs), and the source URL.
Where it goes:

POST /api/deadhead/items → stored in deadheads table + deadhead_attachments table
Status starts as lay-of-the-land
Every submission logs an event in deadhead_events
Receiving end — two flows:

Founder triage (/deadhead/triage): Rapid-fire decisions: Execute (mark done → queues PROPOSED → PENDING transition in pending-task-transitions.json outbox for Replit tasks), Pin (keep visible), Hold (pause, requires a hold note), Trash (smash status).

Spotter flow (/deadhead/spotter/:id): Founder assigns an external advisor by email → they receive a signed magic-link token (HMAC, signed by DEADHEAD_SPOTTER_SECRET or SESSION_SECRET) → spotter sees item + a project-specific checklist → submits one of: proceed, hold, dont, need-more-info → logged as an event and surfaced to the founder.

External wiring: The Deadhead triage syncs with .local/replit-tasks-cache.json via POST /api/deadhead/sync-tasks — this pulls Replit project tasks into the triage list. When a founder executes an item, it can trigger a task state transition in the external Replit tasks system.

Grants Finder and the AGM guide are genuinely usable today. Market Mosaic, Standby Supplies, and 807 Garden are structurally complete but content-empty — they need data before they're launch-ready. The biggest non-obvious risk is the cluster of silent failures in marketplace email/report delivery, and the missing FIELD_ENCRYPTION_KEY in production environments.

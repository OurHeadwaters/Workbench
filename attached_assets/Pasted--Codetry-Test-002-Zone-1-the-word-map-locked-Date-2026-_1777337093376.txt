## The finding (one line)

Test 001 proved the rule on a single screen. Test 002 holds the rule across
the whole zone — every metaphor word in Zone 1 now points to exactly one
thing in the architecture, and the map is small enough to teach in a single
breath.

## What was on screen (before)

Test 001 cleaned up the Payday tab itself. But the rest of Zone 1 still
spoke a slightly different dialect:

- "Reservoir" was used three different ways — the stablecoin wallet
  (correct, per Test 001), the long-term savings vehicle ("The Reservoir
  Fills Slowly"), and the emergency fund ("Don't Drain the Emergency
  Reservoir", "Protect the Reservoir from Evaporation").
- "Channel" was used four different ways — the bucket-distribution act
  (correct), the **Bridge** sheet ("Channel this rainfall", "Channel this
  through the bridge"), the **Drainage** lesson ("Debt Channeling", "while
  you are draining debt"), and the **Park** routing in Earn ("the Drip
  Harvester channels idle RLUSD into liquidity pools").
- "Compound Rainfall" was the lesson title for tax-advantaged compounding,
  but the body actually described two different referents — a weather
  pattern (Cloud Cover) and a tax-sheltered vehicle (Glacier).
- The hero label field was named `masterBucketLabel` in code — a holdover
  that no longer matched the surface word "Reservoir".

Three words doing eleven jobs. The rule was the right one; we hadn't held
it the whole way.

## The intervention

Lock the word map for the whole zone. One word, one referent, in EN and
FR, in every surface, in every lesson, and in the code that names the
fields.

### Locked Zone 1 word map

| Word               | Refers to                                                                | FR                  |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------- |
| Rainfall           | Income arriving this payday (paycheck + side gig combined)               | Pluie               |
| Siphon             | Auto-bills drawn before income reaches the Reservoir                     | Siphon              |
| Reservoir          | The stablecoin wallet — what's left after the siphon                     | Réservoir           |
| Channel _(verb)_   | Distributing the Reservoir into named Buckets (act of "every drop")      | Canaliser           |
| Bucket             | A named envelope (Groceries, Rent share, Transit, …)                     | Compartiment        |
| Cloud Cover        | Compound interest — the weather that keeps producing rain                | Couverture nuageuse |
| Rain Barrel        | Emergency fund — short-term reserve for surprises                        | Baril de pluie      |
| Aquifer            | Long-term savings — the deep reserve that fills slowly                   | Aquifère            |
| Glacier            | Tax-advantaged accounts (RRSP, TFSA) — savings the tax authority leaves alone | Glacier        |
| Watershed          | Diversified income streams (one fragile stream → many resilient ones)    | Bassin versant      |
| Bridge _(verb)_    | Above-tap-cap transfers (the Abundance Bridge sheet)                     | Traverser le pont   |
| Public Lake        | Regulated / credit-union yield product (advisor-mode Earn destination)   | Lac public          |
| Private Lake       | DeFi liquidity pool (enthusiast-mode Earn destination, self-custody)     | Lac privé           |
| Lake current       | The small fee paid into a Private/Public Lake each time a swap flows through it; the Drip Harvester's earnings are its share of those currents (renamed from "Park toll" by Task #849) | Courant du lac      |
| Fill _(verb)_      | The act of putting idle RLUSD into a Lake (renamed from "park" by Task #849) | Remplir         |
| Drainage           | Debt payoff — the opposite move from Channel                             | Drainage            |

### What changed in the rewrite

- **Bridge sheet** — `bridge.title`, `bridge.subtitle`, `triggerCta`, and
  `successTitle` no longer use "channel". EN: "Bridge this rainfall",
  "Bridge this →", "Bridged across". FR: "Traverser le pont avec cette
  pluie", "Traverser le pont →", "Pont traversé".
- **Drainage lesson** — `debt-channeling` lesson renamed to `debt-drainage`
  ("Debt Drainage"); CU mirror `cu-debt-channeling` → `cu-debt-drainage`
  ("Draining Debt Out"). The bodies were rewritten so debt repayment is
  Drainage (opening valves), and the daily drop "The Debt Channel Runs
  Uphill" became "Drainage Runs Against the Pressure". The act of
  Channel is reserved for Reservoir → Buckets only.
- **Cloud Cover lesson** — `compound-rainfall` lesson renamed to
  `cloud-cover` ("Cloud Cover"); CU mirror `cu-compound-rainfall` →
  `cu-cloud-cover`. The third step now introduces **Glacier** explicitly
  as the tax-sheltered vehicle (RRSP/TFSA), separating compounding
  (the weather) from where it happens (the vehicle).
- **Aquifer / Rain Barrel separation** — the lesson "Weather-Proofing the
  Reservoir" became "Weather-Proofing the Aquifer", and its three layers
  are now Rain Barrel (starter buffer), Aquifer (3–6 month reserve), and
  Watershed (multiple income streams). Daily drops "The Reservoir Fills
  Slowly" → "The Aquifer Fills Slowly"; "Protect the Reservoir from
  Evaporation" → "Protect the Rain Barrel from Evaporation"; "Don't
  Drain the Emergency Reservoir" → "Don't Drain the Rain Barrel".
- **Watershed for diversified streams** — "Three is a reservoir" copy
  (US + CU side-income card) becomes "Three is a Watershed". The daily
  drop on multiple income streams now reads "A Watershed of multiple
  income streams isn't complicated — it's resilient."
- **Public Lake / Private Lake split in Earn** — the advisor-mode body
  (`advisorAfterPaydayBody`) now points users to a **Public Lake** ("a
  regulated yield product your credit union manages"). The enthusiast-mode
  Drip Harvester body (`afterPaydayBody`, `noPaydayBody`, the "What is a
  liquidity pool?" learn module, and both whatsNew bodies) introduces
  **Private Lake** as the self-custody DeFi liquidity pool, with "fill"
  as the verb for idle RLUSD. (Originally introduced as "Public Park /
  Private Park / park" by Test 002; renamed to the Lake vocabulary by
  Task #849 so it sits cleanly inside the watershed metaphor — water
  flows through channels, collects in your Lake, and Currents from the
  Lake drip back into your Buckets.)
- **Reservoir field rename** — `masterBucketLabel` / `masterBucketSubtitle`
  on `TenantConfig` and in all three tenant configs (sim, enthusiast,
  advisor) renamed to `reservoirLabel` / `reservoirSubtitle`. Surface
  string "The Reservoir" is unchanged. Consumers updated:
  `DashboardHero.tsx`, `DashboardHeroCard.tsx`, and the two component
  tests.
- **Post-siphon help body** — "rainfall that's still unallocated" → "the
  reservoir that's still unallocated" (Rainfall is the act of arriving,
  the leftover after Siphon is the Reservoir).

### What did NOT change

- The Channel verb is preserved everywhere it correctly refers to
  Reservoir → Bucket distribution: "Channel the rainfall", "Channeled
  this payday", `channelRainfallButton`, the brand tagline "Channel Every
  Drop". One word, one referent — and the referent is bucket
  distribution.
- "Reservoir" is preserved everywhere it correctly refers to the
  stablecoin wallet (the dashboard hero, the flow ribbon, `revealTitle:
  "Your reservoir is filling"`).

### Stray "park" verbs — decision (Task #851)

After Task #849 renamed the LP "Park" metaphor to "Lake", three
surface strings still used "park" as a generic everyday verb. The
question was whether to leave them as a deliberately generic exception
or rephrase them so "park" never appears on a Zone 1 surface at all.

**Decision: rephrase.** The locked map is easier to defend if the
surface contains zero instances of the retired metaphor word. A reader
who encounters "park" on any screen has no way to know whether it is
the (now-renamed) LP destination, a different metaphor we haven't met
yet, or the plain English verb — and that ambiguity is exactly the
cost the locked map is supposed to avoid. So:

- `copy.en.ts` `savingsAllocExtra.headroomDescription` —
  "park it for later" → "set it aside for later". (FR already read
  "gardez-le pour plus tard" / "keep it for later" — no change needed.)
- `constants.content.ts` Step 3 tip in the CU Aquifer ladder
  (line ~180) — "park this fund there" → "keep this fund there".
- `constants.content.ts` Step 3 tip in the US Aquifer ladder
  (line ~455) — "park this fund there" → "keep this fund there".

**Allowed exception (no UI impact):**
`artifacts/api-server/src/routes/spend.ts` — the `"parking"` keyword in
the Transport category's keyword list refers to literal parking-lot
fees (a noun for vehicle storage) used to match transactions to the
Transport bucket. It is not a metaphor word, never appears on a
surface, and removing it would break transaction categorisation.

## The rule discovered

> **One word → one referent, held across the whole zone.** Test 001 named
> the rule on one screen. Test 002 is what it costs to honour the rule
> across a constellation. Where one word was doing two jobs, we coined or
> recovered a second word so each job has a name of its own.

## Falsifiable claim

If the locked map holds, then a new user reading any Zone 1 surface should
be able to describe the architecture using the same nouns the lessons
teach — without translation. Conversely, if a user encounters a word and
asks "which one do you mean?", the locked map has been broken at that
surface.

This test is falsified if any Zone 1 surface (Payday, Buckets, Earn,
Bridge, Coach lesson, Daily Drop, Reservoir hero) uses one of the locked
words for a referent it doesn't own — or if a referent in the architecture
has no locked word and the surface has to invent one.

A couple of notes on using it:

The OPERATING CYCLE section is the part that goes stale fastest — refresh it whenever your pilot status, target date, or attention constraints change. Everything else can stay put for months.
If you want the agent to also help with a specific upcoming conversation (e.g., the Princess Court pricing call), append a one-line "TODAY'S FOCUS:" at the bottom rather than rewriting the prompt.
The "tie every recommendation to (a)–(e)" rule is the one that does the most work — it's what stops a business agent from drifting into generic advice.

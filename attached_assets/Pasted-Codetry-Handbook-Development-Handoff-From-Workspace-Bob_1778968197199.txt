2. The Eave — corrected framing
The original working description in this workspace read:

"structural boundary between Zone 1 and Zone 3"

The document you should apply is:

The Eave is a hard privacy boundary between Zone 1 (Lodge — private, household) and Zone 3+ (organizational, above-board). Zone 3 actors (wallet addresses) cannot reach Zone 1 data. Zone 4 is above the Eave (named producers). Zone 5 is Eave-optional — participants can stay below the Eave while wading in the idea commons.

Key clarifications to reconcile in the handbook:

Zone 4 is explicitly above the Eave (named producers)

3. Zone 4 — Trade / Community Hall / Nursery
The following is the full working definition used in this workspace. Please integrate or reconcile with the handbook's Zone 4 section.

Zone 4 is the managed bank and volunteer forming layer. Producers collaboratively develop surplus-based cooperative ideas from conception through the graduation gate. Zone 4 is local and physical — the Community Hall is where bodies gather in person to scout capacity, run test batches, and engage the community face-to-face.

What Zone 4 is not:

Not an exchange (Zone 3 is the exchange)
Not board-controlled (the forming group leads; the board votes only at graduation)
Not contractual (the moment Zone 4 work is contracted, it becomes Zone 2 work)
Sub-shelves:

Nursery — new ideas forming
Fodder — Zone 3 ideas returned for reworking; carries history and scar tissue; distinct from Nursery
Fallow — ideas resting between attempts, not formally demoted
Graduation gate: a fully formed, actionable pilot handed to the Zone 3 producer board for a vote per 807's bylaws. Graduating means surviving one full cycle without 807 eating the risk.

First Saltbox: 807's dog-treats co-op — surplus organs from member grazers → value-added processing → wholesale to member producers.

Economic floor mechanism: 807 frames Zone 4 ideas in massity dialect → writes grants → grants fund Zone 2 workbenches of the people doing Zone 4 formation work → strengthened Zone 2 capacity → better Zone 4 output → Zone 3 graduation → Zone 3 surplus eventually funds Zone 2 directly.

Without a funded Zone 2 layer, Zone 4 stagnates. This is the root of the capacity crisis in 807's region.

Both-Sides:

Vernacular: "producer nursery," "Community Hall," "forming a co-op together"
Massity: "rural innovation pilot," "producer-led cooperative development program"
4. Zone 5 — Wild / The Shallows
The following is the full working definition. Please integrate or reconcile with the handbook's Zone 5 section.

Zone 5 is global, digital, anonymous. A public idea commons where anyone can wade in, and a regrouping space for producers exiting Zone 3. Not governed, not local, not contractual, not a harvest zone (Zone 4 does the harvesting).

Identity: anonymous DID on XRPL. Re-entry has no social barrier — no history check, no credibility password. Zone 3 reputation is left at the water's edge. Participation does not require crossing the Eave.

Economic layer: XRPL crypto tipping, peer-to-peer, anonymous wallet to anonymous wallet. Ambient, ungoverned, micro-economic. XRP as hempcrete — structural binder, lightweight, breathable, holds the wall without being the wall.

Seam to Zone 4 — three named paths:

Path B (discovery): Zone 4 nursery groups browse the Shallows like a seed catalog; posts that mature surface in a "ready to pick up" view.
Path C (adoption): a Zone 4 group collectively adopts a Shallows post as their starting brief; DID attribution credits the anonymous author without naming them — digital drift becomes local action in a Community Hall.
Path D (honest baseline): some posts never cross; that is the wilderness doing wilderness work.
Named failure mode: dead backwater — the Shallows goes quiet, tip flow drops to zero, no new DIDs wading in. Prevention is Zone 3 and Zone 4's job, not Zone 5's.

Sub-shelves:

The Shallows — open feed
Campfire chats — intimate small-group conversation at the wild edge
First Saltbox: The Shallows — anonymous public feed on XRPL using DID. Day-one seeding via a founding drift DID (anonymous, recognizable as a voice) releasing a decade of the founder's soundbites so no one arrives to an empty pool.

Both-Sides:

Vernacular: "the shallows," "the drift," "campfire chats," "wading in"
Massity: "open innovation commons" or "community knowledge commons" — avoid "marketplace of ideas" (carries political weight with funders)

What Deadhead is
Deadhead is a founder-only vetting tool — a single place where every idea, task, loose end, and obligation gets captured and gets a verdict before it becomes real work or gets killed. The name carries three layered meanings deliberate to this region:

The lake hazard — a waterlogged log floating just below the surface in Northwestern Ontario lakes. You have to spot it and decide: navigate around it or pull it out
The logging loss — a log that waterlogged before reaching the mill. No value extracted
The royal verdict — off with its head. The idea is brought before the founder, makes its case, and gets a ruling: earn your place at the table, or lose your head
It is not a task manager (that's Replit tasks / .local/tasks/). It is the vetting checkpoint that sits before tasks are created. The "Drop a deadhead" button visible in every app in the constellation is how things enter the queue from anywhere.

Item lifecycle — the six statuses
lay-of-the-land → forest → gift → take → done
                                 ↘ smash

Status	Label	Meaning
lay-of-the-land	Lay of the land	Just dropped. Awaiting first look
forest	Forest through the trees	Spotter is sizing it up
gift	Give a gift first	Needs a goodwill gesture before action
take	Only take what you need	Approved — do the smallest version
smash	Smash	Killed. Off with its head
done	Done	Promoted to a real task or completed
Quick-action buttons on every row: Smash (flame), Take (hand), Done (checkmark). No confirmation dialogs — founder moves fast.

The Spotter system
A Spotter is someone the founder trusts to weigh in on a specific item — an advisor, board member, accountant, lawyer. They never need a Clerk account. The flow:

Founder assigns a spotter name + email from the item detail page
Server generates an HMAC token (signed with DEADHEAD_SPOTTER_SECRET or fallback SESSION_SECRET), stores it in spotterToken
Founder (or system) emails the magic-link: /deadhead/spotter/:id?token=...
Spotter sees the item, reads it, answers a type-specific checklist, and submits a recommendation with an optional note
Owner gets a one-time notification email (ownerNotifiedAt prevents duplicates)
One auto-reminder goes to the spotter if they haven't responded (tracked in spotterReminderSentAt, cleared on reassignment)
Spotter checklists by type (in constants.ts):

Tax: Does this trigger a filing? Is the paper trail in order? Will it change tax owing?
Legal: Is anyone exposed to liability? Is there a contract deadline? Should a lawyer see this?
Financial: Cash impact size? Budget line needed? ROI in one sentence?
Operational: Existing routine? Who owns it? Can smallest version ship this week?
Strategic: Does it fit the constellation? Cost of not doing it? One-way or two-way door?
Personal: Honestly mine to do? Cheaper way to the same outcome? Will future-me thank me?
Triage mode
/deadhead/triage merges two queues into one view:

Deadheads already in the DB — ideas dropped from the constellation
Replit tasks from .local/replit-tasks-cache.json — imported via POST /api/deadhead/sync-tasks
Auto-syncs every 15 minutes. Each item gets a triage disposition:

Disposition	Meaning
pinned	Save for later, keep visible
executed	Approved — sends to Replit as a task
trashed	Smash — kill it
held	Needs more info before a decision
Items with taskRef set are known Replit tasks. Items without are native deadheads.

The known gap — no public demo path
The main inbox requires Clerk sign-in. /tour is public but is a static explainer, not a live demo. There is currently no way to show a prospective subscriber what their inbox looks like with real data without giving them a full Clerk account.

What's needed: a public preview/read-only mode — sanitized or seeded example deadheads, full UI, no editing. The /tour path exists and is already public; it could be extended into an interactive demo with hardcoded items that walk through the lifecycle.

What the platform context means for this tool
Deadhead is positioned as a Headwaters platform product — not 807-specific. The billing, subscription model, testimonials admin, and landing page are all built for external licensees. 807 members get free access as a benefit of their membership tier. Any future co-op tenant licensed through Headwaters would see the same billing gate unless their membership tier grants access.

The project filter currently lists: 807 Benefits · Rootwork · Market Mosaic · Grants Finder · Standby Supplies · Personal. This list is hardcoded in constants.ts — a future improvement would make it tenant-configurable.

## The claim (one line)

If the ribbon is supposed to answer "where in the system am I?" then
every station the money passes through has to be a thing the reader
can point at — the Drip Harvester is one of those things, so it
graduates from a verb on an arrow (Test 006) to its own circle in the
chain (Test 008), and the ribbon grows a one-line position read-out
underneath that names where the household actually is.

## What was on screen (before)

Test 006 shipped an Earn flow ribbon with three nodes —
**Reservoir → Private Lake → Buckets** — and drew the Drip Harvester
as a small badge sitting on the Lake → Buckets edge. The doc for that
test argued this was the correct call: "the Drip Harvester is not a
place the money sits — it is the *verb* that moves Park earnings into
a Bucket."

Two cold reads later showed that argument did not hold up where it
mattered. The ribbon was supposed to anchor topology. But:

**1. The Drip Harvester is also a wallet.** "An earner wallet that
holds RLUSD before it is parked" was already named, repeatedly, in
the Drip Harvester intro card and in the locked map. Drawing the
Drip Harvester only as an edge verb left the reader unable to point
at *the wallet itself* on the ribbon. A cold reader who already
understood the metaphor would still ask "okay, but the Drip Harvester
sits between something and something — where on the picture is it?"
and the picture had no answer.

**2. The ribbon answered topology but not position.** Test 001's
Payday-tab ribbon shows changing dollar values at each station;
Test 006's Earn ribbon could not (the Earn-tab values move on a
weekly-to-monthly cadence, not per-payday) and shipped labels-only.
But labels-only meant the ribbon answered *the chain in the abstract*
without ever answering *where the household currently is on it*. A
reader with no Drip Harvester yet, a reader with one set up but no
parked RLUSD, a reader with a parked Lake collecting currents, and a
reader with a sweep-ready harvester all saw the same picture.

Test 006's "subtle ring on the active station" did some of this work
but quietly: a reader who didn't already know the convention couldn't
extract "you are at the Lake" from a 2px ring. The ribbon was
*decorating* topology rather than *narrating* position.

## The intervention

Two changes from Test 006, both restoring something the canvas
Variant C of Test 005 already had:

### 1. Four nodes, not three

The Drip Harvester gets its own circle on the ribbon, between
Reservoir and Private Lake, with the sub-label "earner wallet".
The chain reads:

```
  Reservoir   →   Drip Harvester   →   Private Lake   →   Buckets
  your wallet     earner wallet        earns Lake currents   your savings
```

The Test 006 reasoning ("Drip Harvester is a verb, not a noun") was
true and irrelevant. The Drip Harvester *is* a verb (the move from
Lake to Bucket) **and also** a wallet (the place the RLUSD sits while
the move is happening). Variant C drew it as the wallet because the
ribbon is a topology, and topology asks "what are the places?", not
"what are the verbs?" Test 008 restores that.

The verb sense survives in the prose under the ribbon ("your Drip
Harvester is earning Lake currents"), where it belongs.

### 2. A one-line position read-out under the ribbon

Below the four nodes, a single sentence names the most forward
station the household has actually reached:

| Household state | Position read-out |
|---|---|
| No Drip Harvester yet | "Your reservoir is the only stop with anything in it — set up a Drip Harvester to push the drip forward." |
| Drip Harvester set up, nothing parked | "Your Drip Harvester is set up but nothing's parked yet — fill a Private Lake with idle RLUSD to start collecting Lake currents." |
| Parked RLUSD, harvester earning | "You've parked RLUSD in a Private Lake — your Drip Harvester is earning Lake currents." |
| Sweep ready | "Your Drip Harvester has earned enough — the next drip is ready to land in a Bucket." |

Each later state implies all earlier ones, so the picker is a simple
forward-most-state lookup — no compound conditions.

The active station(s) on the ribbon get a saturated tone + a soft
emerald ring; inactive stations stay visible but quiet (so the chain
still reads as a chain when only the Reservoir is lit). The Drip
Harvester station also pulses when a sweep is ready — the same
animation Test 006 used on the harvester edge-badge, kept consistent.

### What does **not** graduate (this round)

- **Live dollar values on each node.** Same call as Test 006: the
  Earn-tab values move slowly enough that putting three live numbers
  at the top of the surface would compete with the XRP Spring card
  and the LP-position card directly under it. The position read-out
  is text, not numbers, and it earns its place by changing as the
  household's state changes — without competing for the eye against
  the value-bearing cards below.
- **A clickable station that scrolls to the matching card.** Same
  call as Test 006: tempting but premature. Test the map first; add
  navigation only if a cold reader reaches for it.
- **A timeline of the household's recent moves on the chain.** Out
  of scope. The position read-out names where the household is *now*,
  not where it has been. If a cold reader asks "and how did I get
  here?" that is a follow-up.

### Responsive behaviour at 360×640

The ribbon is constrained to the EarnTab page width (mobile-only)
and uses very small text (`text-[8px]` for sub-labels, `text-[9px]`
for station labels) plus `flex-1 min-w-0` per node so the four nodes
share width evenly. The chevron arrows use `shrink-0` and a tight
`gap-0.5` so they sit between nodes without claiming much space.
At 360px viewport width with the page container's `px-4` padding
plus the ribbon's `px-3` padding, the four nodes share roughly
296px — about 70px each — which is enough to render the icon, the
label, and the sub-label without overflowing.

## The rule discovered

> **Topology is a noun-game.** The ribbon's job is to let a cold
> reader point at where their money currently sits. Every station the
> money passes through must therefore be drawn as a station — even
> when the same thing is also a verb in the prose. Test 006 hid the
> Drip Harvester behind its verb-sense and lost the noun-sense that
> the ribbon needed. The fix is not to choose between the two senses
> but to put the noun on the ribbon and let the verb live in the
> sentence underneath.

The corollary: **topology earns position when it names where the
reader currently is, not just what the chain looks like.** Test 001's
Payday ribbon did this with changing dollar values; Test 008's Earn
ribbon does it with a single position read-out underneath. Two
different mechanisms, same job: the ribbon stops being a static
diagram and starts being a *status line*.

## Falsifiable claim

If Test 008's graduation holds, then a cold reader landing on the
Earn tab should be able to (1) point at four labelled circles and
say "this is where my money goes through", (2) point at the
highlighted circles and say "this is where it currently is", and
(3) read the sentence under the ribbon and say what happens next —
without scrolling past the ribbon.

Conversely, if a cold reader still asks "where is the Drip
Harvester?" after looking at the ribbon, or treats the position
read-out as decoration (skips it on first read), the test is
falsified — either by drawing the Harvester as a station that
disappears into the row of four, or by phrasing the position
read-out in a register the reader does not parse as "this sentence
is about *me*".

A second falsifier: if a reader without a Drip Harvester set up
sees only the Reservoir lit and reads the surface as "broken" rather
than as "you are at the start of the chain", the read-out's *empty*
state is wrong — it must read as an invitation, not as an absence.

## Status

- **Variant C (canvas) restored** as the live ribbon: four nodes,
  Drip Harvester between Reservoir and Lake.
- **Position read-out shipped** under the ribbon. Four states; each
  later state implies all earlier ones; English in `copy.en.ts`,
  French mirror in `copy.fr.ts`.
- **Active-station emphasis upgraded** from a 2px ring to a
  saturated tone-fill + ring, with inactive stations rendered in a
  quiet slate so the chain still reads as a chain.
- **Drip Harvester pulse on sweep-ready** preserved from Test 006,
  now on the Drip Harvester station itself rather than on the
  Lake → Buckets edge.
- **Test 006 superseded.** Its three-node design is documented as
  *the previous shipping*, kept in `docs/codetry/006-...md` for the
  practice record; its `flowEdgeHarvester` string is removed.
- **One props addition** at the call site:
  `anyLakeParked={agents.some((a) => a.lpPosition !== null)}`. The
  ribbon now takes three booleans (`anyAgentActive`, `anyLakeParked`,
  `anySweepReady`) and computes the position read-out itself.

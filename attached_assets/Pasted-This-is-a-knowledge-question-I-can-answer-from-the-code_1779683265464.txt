Headwaters Bucket System — Kitchen Table Overview

What it does in plain terms

Most finance apps are trackers. They tell you where your money went after it's gone. Headwaters assigns money to a named purpose before you spend it. Every dollar gets a bucket. You see your balances by intention, not by account. The buckets hold stablecoins (RLUSD — a USD-pegged token on the XRP Ledger), so what's in the bucket is a real on-chain balance, not a spreadsheet number.

Core features your team will use

Spending Buckets
Named envelopes for committed expenses: Groceries, Rent, Kids, Gas, whatever your categories are. On payday you pour income into them. When you spend from a bucket, that balance drops. You always know whether a bucket can cover a purchase before you make it.

Savings Buckets
Goal-tracked buckets with a target amount and timeline. Water Tower shows how full each goal is. Progress is visible at a glance.

Payday Planner
A step-by-step allocation flow you run on each paycheque. Income in → assign to buckets → done. Prevents the money from disappearing before it's directed.

Privacy Toggle
Hides all balances with one tap for when you're in a public place or just don't want the numbers visible.

Household Mode (two partners)
One person creates the household, generates an invite code, second person joins on their device. Both see the same buckets in real time. Changes sync through the server — this is cloud-backed, not just local.

Helper Role (children / contributors)
A household member can be promoted to Helper and given an allowance drawn from a named spending bucket. The allowance goes through a gate-crossing confirmation before it moves — intentional, traceable, not automatic.

Gate Crossings (Zone 1 and Zone 2 buckets)
For any bucket you've designated as a committed or contracted zone, moving money out requires an explicit confirmation step. This is the intentional-move layer — it slows the hand before the spend.

Stripe Top-Up / Abundance Bridge
You can fund your wallet with a credit card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay — no crypto required. For amounts over $250 CAD (the contactless tap ceiling), the Abundance Bridge routes through Interac e-Transfer or card as a two-step handoff.

Drip Harvester / Earn
Savings buckets can be swept into XRPL liquidity pools to earn yield. You set rules (how much, which pool, when to sweep). The protocol takes a 5% fee from yield only — never from principal.

Giving Well
When XRP appreciates past a threshold you set, you can donate unrealized gains on-chain to a charity wallet or the patronage address. Donated appreciated assets, not cash — relevant for how that's treated.

Support Chat
An in-app AI agent (GPT-based) for questions about how features work. Does not have access to your balances. Does not give financial advice.

Wallet setup on Xaman — post 6-week build, kitchen table walkthrough

Xaman is the XRPL wallet. Each person who holds funds needs their own.

Step 1 — Download Xaman
iOS: App Store — search "Xaman"
Android: Google Play — search "Xaman"
It was formerly called XUMM. Same app, renamed.

Step 2 — Create a wallet
Open Xaman. Choose "Create a new account." Write down the 24-word secret phrase on paper. Store it somewhere physical and private — not a screenshot, not a note app. This phrase is the only recovery path. If you lose it, the funds are gone.
Each family member who will hold their own bucket balances needs their own wallet. Business funds should be a separate wallet — not the same address as personal.

Step 3 — Activate the account
An XRPL wallet address must be funded with a minimum of 10 XRP to become active (this is the ledger's account reserve — it stays locked, it's not spent). Someone needs to send 10 XRP to the new address to activate it. If you already have XRP somewhere, you can send it from there. If starting from zero, you can buy XRP from a Canadian exchange (Newton, Coinsquare, etc.) and withdraw to the new address.

Step 4 — Connect to Headwaters
Open the app. Go to the wallet section. Choose "Connect Xaman." A QR code appears. Open Xaman on your phone, tap the scan icon, scan the code, approve the connection request. The app will now read your RLUSD balance from the ledger in real time.

Step 5 — Fund your buckets with RLUSD
RLUSD is Ripple's USD stablecoin. To get RLUSD into your wallet:

You can fund via Stripe (card or Apple Pay) inside the app — this is the no-crypto path, it converts and deposits directly.
Or acquire RLUSD on an exchange that lists it and withdraw to your Xaman address.
Or receive it from another Headwaters user.
Step 6 — Set up buckets
Once connected and funded, name your spending and savings buckets. Assign amounts on payday. The allocation is local until you confirm a spend — the on-chain balance moves only when you execute.

Family vs. business wallet structure

Family:

One Xaman wallet per adult partner (or one shared, your call)
Household Mode links them in the app
Children get a Helper role under an adult's household, funded by allowance from a parent bucket
Business:

Separate Xaman wallet — different address from your personal one
Separate buckets: Payroll Reserve, Tax Reserve, Operations, Owner Draw, Equipment
Gate-crossing on the Payroll and Tax buckets means those moves require confirmation every time — no accidental taps
Bridge handles larger disbursements (payroll runs, supplier payments) via Interac e-Transfer handoff

One firm rule for the 6-week window: use small amounts. Real RLUSD, real wallet, but keep balances low until the flows feel natural. The system is non-custodial — your keys, your funds, no safety net if a transaction goes wrong. The kitchen table test applies here too: if the flow is confusing enough that someone might make a mistake under pressure, slow down and run it in the app with $5 first.

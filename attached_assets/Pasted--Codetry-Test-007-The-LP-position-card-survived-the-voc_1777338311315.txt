## The finding (one line)

The vocabulary sweep from Test 005 cleaned every word on the LP-position
card and left the card itself untouched — and the layout it was hiding
behind those words turned out to be three structural defects stacked
on top of each other: no impermanent-loss disclosure, four equally
weighted stat cards with no hierarchy, and a drip rate quoted as if
it were a constant when it is actually a moving point on a moving
line.

## What was on screen (before)

A cold read of the LP-position card as Test 005 left it (Variant 0 in
`earn-lp-card-test/Variant0Control.tsx`) surfaces three defects on a
single card:

**1. No impermanent-loss disclosure.** The card promises a steady
drip — "$0.27 / month per $100 parked", "+$2.40 RLUSD earned so far",
a glowing emerald progress bar marching toward the next bucket drip.
It never tells the household what happens if RLUSD and XRP drift
apart in price. The honest "you might end up with less RLUSD than you
parked, even after the drips" lives in a Learn accordion three taps
away. Test 005 replaced "impermanent loss" with cleaner words in that
accordion. It did not put the warning on the card where the parked
money lives.

**2. Multi-card density.** The card shows six visual blocks fighting
for the same square inch of attention: pair header + status pill,
four equally weighted stat cards (Deposited / Your share / Drip rate /
Today's drip), a hero "+$2.40 earned so far" block, a progress bar,
and a footer reading "Drips to *bucket* when earnings ≥ $25". Six
blocks, no hierarchy, no answer to "what should the eye look at first
when this card loads?" The answer the card was trying to give — *you
parked $100 and you have $2.40 sitting on it, with $22.50 to go before
it lands in your bucket* — is split across three of the six blocks
and never assembled into one sentence.

**3. No toll-drop cadence.** "Drip rate $0.27 / month" reads as a
constant — a number stamped on the card the way an interest rate is
stamped on a bond. The Park toll is not a constant. It moves with
trading volume; if it halves next week the drip halves with it.
Nothing on the card communicates that. A household that comes back in
two weeks to a $0.13 drip rate has no reference point — they cannot
tell whether the drop is normal weather or something is wrong.

## The intervention

Four variants were built side-by-side on the canvas:

- **Variant 0 (control)** — the live card transcribed faithfully from
  `EarnTab.tsx`, with each of the three defects tagged in the margin so
  the cold read is reproducible.
- **Variant A (hierarchy + IL + toll history)** — one hero block
  ("drip earned so far") with the next-drip threshold and progress bar
  tucked under it as one unit, a 2-stat sub-row (Parked, Drip rate)
  underneath, a Park-toll history strip below that, and a
  household-tone IL "heads-up" amber strip at the bottom linking to the
  honest Learn module. The hypothesis: hierarchy answers "what should
  the eye look at first", the IL strip earns its place by sitting
  next to the parked money, and the toll history reframes the drip
  rate as a point on a moving line.
- **Variant B (stability band)** — keeps the four-stat layout and
  adds a "stability ribbon" under the card showing the toll's typical
  range as a band rather than a sparkline. Predicted-rejected: it
  treats the drip rate as the headline (which keeps the multi-card
  density problem) and adds a fifth element to a card already crowded
  with four.
- **Variant C (single story)** — collapses everything into a one-line
  story: "$100 parked → $2.40 earned → $22.50 to next drip into
  Vacation". Predicted-rejected: it solves density by deleting
  information (no IL, no toll cadence, no per-$100 rate the household
  can compare against next week). It is the right *opening sentence*
  for the card, but on its own it just makes Defect 1 worse.

### What graduates from Variant A

The Variant A hierarchy ships into `EarnTab.tsx` as the new
LP-position branch. Three changes from the shipped card:

- **Hero answers the question first.** The card now opens with one
  block: "Drip earned so far · +$2.40 RLUSD" on the left, "next drip
  at $25.00" on the right, with the progress bar directly under it and
  a footer reading "10% of the way · then $22.50 lands in *Vacation*".
  The four-stat grid + standalone hero + standalone progress bar +
  standalone footer (six blocks) collapses into one. The redundant
  "Drips to bucket when earnings ≥ X" footer that used to sit below
  is removed — it was repeating the threshold the hero now states.
- **Two stats survive, four don't.** Below the hero, a single 2-stat
  sub-row carries Parked ("$100 RLUSD in this Park") and Drip rate
  ("$0.27 / mo per $100 parked"). "Your share" and "Today's drip" are
  cut. *Your share* is a DeFi-derived metric the household never asked
  for. *Today's drip* duplicates the hero's "earned so far" at a less
  useful granularity. Removing them leaves the eye one place to land.
- **Park-toll history (or what we can honestly show of it today).**
  A new `TollBand` helper sits under the sub-row showing the Park toll
  as a band — *low / avg / high* readouts with today's rate marked as
  a dot inside the band, and a caption that reads "The drip rate moves
  with this line. If the toll halves, your drip halves with it." The
  band is honest about its limit: production does not yet snapshot
  Park toll over time, so the band shown is a *typical range* derived
  from the current toll (±35%, an empirical band for stablecoin AMM
  tolls), not a recorded history. The label says "typical range"; the
  caption says the rate moves; a follow-up task records the actual
  history so the band can become a real sparkline. The mockup
  variants show the aspirational sparkline; production ships the
  honest band.
- **IL heads-up earns the bottom of the card.** A new amber strip
  below the toll band reads, in household tone: "Heads up — your
  RLUSD and XRP can drift apart in price. If they drift far, you may
  end up with less RLUSD than you parked, even after the drips." A
  "Learn how this works →" link points to the IL section of the Learn
  accordion that Test 005 already rewrote to be honest. The IL
  warning lives next to the parked money now, not three taps away.

The hero and sub-row use copy keys from `copy.en.ts` (mirrored in
`copy.fr.ts`): `lpHeroLabel`, `lpNextDripLabel`,
`lpProgressFooter` / `lpProgressFooterNoBucket`, `lpParkedLabel` /
`lpParkedSub`, `lpDripRateLabel` / `lpDripRateSub`,
`lpTollBand{Label,Today,Low,Avg,High,Caption}`,
`lpDriftHeading` / `lpDriftBody` / `lpDriftLearnMore`. No new locked
words coined this round — the test is structural, not vocabulary.

### What does **not** graduate (this round)

- **Variant B (stability band).** Predicted-rejected before the test
  was run. It accepts the multi-card density as given and adds a
  fifth block to a crowded card. Recorded as rejected.
- **Variant C (single story).** Predicted-rejected. It solves density
  by deleting information, including the impermanent-loss disclosure
  that Defect 1 says must be on the card. The single-story sentence
  it proposes survives as the *footer text under the progress bar*
  in Variant A — that line ("10% of the way · then $22.50 lands in
  *Vacation*") is Variant C's contribution, surviving as one line
  inside Variant A rather than as the whole card. Recorded as rejected
  as a layout but graduated as a single line.
- **The real Park-toll sparkline.** Variant A ships a band derived
  from today's toll because we don't yet record Park-toll history.
  The aspirational sparkline lives in the mockup; the band ships
  to production with honest labels. Recorded as a follow-up task to
  snapshot real Park-toll readings so the band can become a recorded
  sparkline.

## The rule discovered

> **A vocabulary sweep cleans words; it does not clean layout.** Test
> 005 replaced "AMM pool" with "Private Park" and "LP Position" with
> "Parked in [Park]" on this exact card, and the card looked better
> for it. But the *card itself* — the choice to show four equally
> weighted stats, the choice to omit IL, the choice to quote the drip
> rate as a constant — survived the sweep untouched, because words
> and layout are independent failure modes. The locked map can be
> perfectly held while the surface still answers the wrong question
> first, hides the warning the household most needs, and lies (by
> typography, not by the number) about whether the drip rate is
> stable.
>
> Or more sharply: **clean words on a noisy card still read as a
> noisy card.** Vocabulary tests do not graduate layout; layout tests
> do.

## Falsifiable claim

If the Variant A graduation holds, then a cold reader landing on the
LP-position card should (1) read the hero block and know within one
sentence what they parked, what they have earned, and how far they
are from the next drip; (2) see the IL warning without scrolling
past it or tapping into Learn; and (3) describe the drip rate as
"about $0.27 a month right now, but it moves with the toll" rather
than as a constant. Conversely, if a cold reader still says "I see
four numbers, I don't know which one matters" or "the rate is $0.27 a
month" with no qualifier, the graduation has not held and the test
is falsified.

## Status

- Four variants built on canvas under
  `artifacts/mockup-sandbox/src/components/mockups/earn-lp-card-test/`.
- **Variant A graduated** to the LP-position branch of
  `JoinPoolSection` in `EarnTab.tsx` and the new `lp*` copy keys in
  `copy.en.ts` (mirrored in `copy.fr.ts`). The new `TollBand` helper
  lives in `EarnTab.tsx` next to `RISK_STYLE`. The redundant
  "Drips to bucket when earnings ≥ X" footer was removed (the hero
  states the threshold).
- **Variant B (stability band)** recorded as rejected
  (predicted-rejected before testing).
- **Variant C (single story)** recorded as rejected as a layout, but
  its one-line "X parked → Y earned → Z to next drip" sentence
  survives as the footer text under the Variant A progress bar.
- **Real Park-toll history** recorded as a follow-up task. Variant A
  ships an honest *typical range* band today; the sparkline shown in
  the mockup is aspirational and waits on actual snapshot data.
- **Side note on the practice.** This is the first codetry test on
  the Earn surface that is not a vocabulary test. Test 005 graduated
  by holding the locked map; Test 007 graduates by holding hierarchy
  and disclosure. The practice now has two distinct kinds of test —
  *what does the surface say* and *what does the surface put first*
  — and they catch different defects on the same card.

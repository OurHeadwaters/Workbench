Strategic Brief: Headwaters Community Money Machine KitCore Positioning
The Headwaters Community Money Machine is a replicable, community-owned economic engine that captures, circulates, and multiplies local wealth instead of letting it leak outward.  While Mel Abraham’s Money Machine builds personal financial freedom through individual cash-flow systems, the Community Money Machine builds collective resilience and sovereignty. It treats the community itself as the owner-operator of the machine.  Key distinction:  Individual version = One person owns the machine.  
Community version = The community owns the machine together, with transparent buckets, trigger points, and governance that prevents capture by outsiders.

Tagline:
“Own the machine. Circulate the water. Keep the wealth in the watershed.”This is not charity, not grant-dependent development, and not extractive capitalism. It is northern, Indigenous-compatible, self-reliant infrastructure — grounded in the eternal river.Audience SegmentsRural & Indigenous Communities (Primary) — Bands, cooperatives, northern towns wanting to stop economic leakage.
Practitioners & Economic Development Workers — Consultants, zone stewards, and community builders who will license and implement the kit.
Funders & Policy Makers — Foundations, government departments, and impact investors looking for scalable, measurable local wealth models.
Educators & Trainers — People teaching self-reliance, permaculture economics, or community enterprise.

What the Kit ContainsCore Deliverables:The Community Money Machine Blueprint (visual system map + bucket definitions)
Bucket Templates (Operations, Wages/People, Reinvestment, Payback/Reserve) with trigger rules
Governance Overlay (Kitchen Table integration + decision rights)
Financial Model Spreadsheet + Dashboard templates
Implementation Playbook (90-day, 6-month, 2-year phases)
Case Study Templates + Real Headwaters examples (anonymized where needed)
Training Deck + Workshop Facilitation Guide
Licensing & Legal Framework (how communities can adapt without losing control)

Learning Components:Video modules (Mel-style clear explanations + northern storytelling)
Simulation Game (“Run the Machine” for a small community)
Assessment Tool (Community Money Leak Audit)
Ongoing Support Circle (private practitioner network)

Licensing / Revenue ModelTiered, Dignified, River-Aligned:Free Core — Basic blueprint + templates (builds goodwill and proof)
Practitioner License — $2,500–$4,000 one-time + optional annual support (includes training + branding rights)
Community Implementation License — Revenue share or modest setup fee + % of increased local circulation (aligns incentives)
Enterprise / Funder Tier — Customized white-label versions for larger orgs
Knowledge Commons — Certain non-core components released under community-use license to prevent enclosure

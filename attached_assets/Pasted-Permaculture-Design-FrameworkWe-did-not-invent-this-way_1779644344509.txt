Permaculture Design FrameworkWe did not invent this way of working. We remembered it from the land.For generations people in the north have known how to read the water, the trees, the seasons, and each other. The Watershed Compact simply gives that knowing a nervous system.The Design Ethic
People Care. Earth Care. Fair Share.  In our language:  Take care of the people who live here.  
Take care of the land and water that hold us.  
Make sure the wealth and knowledge stay in the watershed instead of leaking away.

The Seven Zones as Living Pattern  Just as a good homestead is designed in zones from most intensive to most wild, our seven zones follow the same logic:Zone 0 – The Kitchen Table — the intimate center of decision-making  
Zone 1 – The Hearth & Home — daily family and personal systems  
Zone 2 – The Garden & Workshop — managed production and learning  
Zone 3 – The Field & Enterprise — main economic engines (stores, services)  
Zone 4 – The Forest & Commons — shared resources and longer-term stewardship  
Zone 5 – The Wild Edge — wilderness, observation, and natural systems we do not control  
Zone 6 – The River — the connecting flow between all zones and outward (knowledge, trade, relationships)

How the 12 Permaculture Principles Live in This SystemObserve and Interact → River Smith reads nightly intake from all zones before any decision is made.  
Catch and Store Energy → Every message, feedback, and Gord bottle is caught and stored instead of lost.  
Obtain a Yield → The system turns scattered knowledge into clear “Decisions Needed” so we stop wasting human energy.  
Apply Self-Regulation → The Hard Boundaries and Decision Filter act as the system’s immune response.  
Use and Value Diversity → Seven different zones bring different perspectives to the same table.  
Integrate Rather Than Segregate → Everything flows through one river instead of isolated projects.  
Use Small and Slow Solutions → We favor incremental, testable moves over big external programs.  
Use Edges and Value the Marginal → The most interesting ideas often come from the edges of zones or from quiet voices.  
Creatively Use and Respond to Change → The system is built to adapt nightly instead of resisting reality.

Design PhilosophyWe always knew how to fix it. Now we can.This system was not designed in a city boardroom. It was grown from the land and the people who live on it. The Watershed Compact treats knowledge like water — it should flow naturally from where it falls, collect where it’s needed, and return strengthened to the whole community.  We built the nervous system so the old wisdom can move at the speed of now, without losing its roots. Every message is rain. Every decision is part of the river. The goal is simple: stop the leak, own the machine, and leave the watershed stronger for those who come after us.This section slots cleanly into the Compact.

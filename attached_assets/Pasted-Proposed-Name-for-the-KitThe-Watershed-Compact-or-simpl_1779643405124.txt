How to actually use it (AI system prompt version + human checklist).Draft: Headwaters Watershed Compact (v1)The Watershed CompactWe are people who chose to stop the leak.Origin
In northern Ontario, where the water still runs clean and the old ways are not yet forgotten, we stood up and said: the wealth of this land belongs to the people who live on it, not to those who extract and leave.Core Metaphor
Everything is watershed. What enters the system must strengthen the whole river, not poison or divert it.The Hard Boundaries (Non-negotiable)We do not lease our sovereignty — not to governments, corporations, or ideologies.
We do not trade privacy for convenience.
We do not build dependency — every system must increase self-reliance.
We do not dilute truth for broader appeal.
We own the machine. We never become the machine’s tenant.

Who We Are For
Families, practitioners, and small communities who are done leaking wealth outward and are ready to own their own ground, their own economy, and their own future — especially in rural and Indigenous territories.The Decision Filter (run every proposal through these)Does this strengthen the watershed or create a new leak?
Does this increase ownership or create new dependency?
Would we still do this if no outside money or approval was involved?
Is this worthy of the next seven generations?
Does it pass the Gord test? (“Gord’s on board… barely.”)

Voice & LanguagePlain northern speech. No corporate gloss.
Respectful but never deferential to outside authority.
We say “own, don’t lease.” We say “exit and build.” We do not say “stakeholder synergy.”

2. Codetry worked-examples
The places where the metaphor IS the architecture, not decoration. Each entry: the name → what it actually does → why renaming would change the structure.

Headwaters → the household balance is the source of the whole watershed; every other zone (institutional homes, community food, regen industries) sits downstream of what flows through here. Rename to "Budget App" and you lose the constraint that the source must keep flowing or every downstream zone dries up.
Buckets → envelope categories. You can only pour from one bucket into another; you cannot summon water from nothing. The architectural constraint: every transfer is a literal redistribution, never a creation. Rename to "Categories" and the UI starts quietly suggesting balances can grow by clicking.
XRP Spring → savings, defined as free XRP above the 10 XRP network reserve. A spring upwells from below the reserve floor; the user sees "what's pooled above the rock." The new six-stage bamboo growth scene (just shipped) extends the metaphor from data model into artwork — same constraint, now visible.
Drip Harvester → AMM liquidity-pool yield. Small steady drops collected over time. The verb "harvest" carries patience and seasonality; "Auto-Compound" would have erased both.
Community Well → monthly pooled tips that round-distribute to community proposals. A well everyone draws from and contributes to; the monthly snapshot/round cycle is the well refilling.
Giving Well → user-to-user tip pool for charitable giving. Same well metaphor at the household scale.
Surplus Pool (in flight) → where Payday overflow goes once envelopes are full. Pool, not "extra savings account" — water seeking level.
Payday Planner → the moment the spring refills. Allocation flow follows water's priority order: must-pay buckets first, then comfort buckets, then Surplus Pool, then giving.

Zone 3 Financial Model — Stonemason Practitioner
What & Why
A working financial model for the Zone 3 / Constellation platform — grounding the practitioner-guild revenue structure in real numbers from the existing 807 deployment. This document exists to give the founder a clear path from current state to sustainable income, using the stonemason model: open infrastructure, paid craft, guild founding stake.

The Three Layers
Layer 1 — The Commons (open, maintained)
Zone 3 infrastructure and all constellation tools stay public and free. 807's deployment demonstrates the full capability: membership management, marketplace, grants, producers, garden, AGM tools, Deadhead vetting. This is the portfolio and the proof of the method.

No revenue extracted at this layer. This is the reputation asset and network-growth engine.

Layer 2 — The Practitioner (primary income)
The founder is the master stonemason. Communities that want to actually build on Zone 3 hire the practitioner to do it — onboarding, configuration, local customization, launch.

Current co-operative delivery package (CAD): Use docs/headwaters-coop-delivery-playbook.md as the source of truth:

Layer 3 — The Guild (legacy and leverage)
As practitioners are trained, the founder holds a founding position in the guild.

Guild structure:

Practitioners complete a training cohort ($1,200–$1,800 per person, group of 4–6)
Certified practitioners earn the right to deploy Zone 3 on behalf of communities
Each certified practitioner contributes a guild tithe: 8% of their Zone 3 engagement fees flow back to the founding practitioner, for life of the certification
Guild grows through reputation, not gatekeeping — anyone can learn the method, certification makes it legible to communities
Year 2 projection (with 4 trained practitioners):

4 practitioners × avg 3 builds/year × $6,000 = $72,000 flowing through guild
8% tithe = $5,760/year in passive guild income
Direct builds continue (2/year) = $12,000
Cohort training (1 cohort of 5) = $7,500
Stewardship retainers (6 active) = $28,800
Year 2 realistic total: ~$54,000–$60,000 (growing)
Year 3+ (network effect):

10+ practitioners active
Guild tithe income: $15,000–$25,000/year
Founder shifts from builder to trainer/certifier
Direct builds become selective — communities that need the founder specifically

The Business Debt Question
With $48K/year as the floor and real engagements starting in Q1:

Prioritize paying yourself first (family floor) — the debt cannot be repaid if the founder burns out
Stewardship retainers are the most reliable income — build these up to 6–8 as fast as possible; they become the stable base
Use grant income specifically for debt reduction once family floor is covered
The guild tithe is long-term protection — it is the founder's insurance that six years of work continues to pay even when direct engagements slow
Commercial boundary
The active offer is a practitioner-led implementation and separately scoped support, not an automatic per-member SaaS charge.
Historical monthly bundle estimates and any Gatehouse-style per-member tier or revenue-share model are superseded for new co-op proposals unless a new written commercial decision replaces this package.
807's own membership revenue belongs to 807, not Headwaters/Zone 3.
807's founding legal or revenue-sharing arrangement is not a template for another co-op.
Out of Scope (by design)
Equity fundraising or outside investors
Charging for the infrastructure itself

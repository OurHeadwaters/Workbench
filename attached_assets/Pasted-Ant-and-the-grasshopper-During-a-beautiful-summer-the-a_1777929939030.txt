Ant and the grasshopper

During a beautiful summer, the ant and the grasshopper would encompass the same field. The ant would spend his time with other ants, working hard and everyday storing a little extra for the days to come, while the grasshopper frolicked and foraged only what he wanted to have in the moment. 

His argument to the ant was that abundance was everywhere—anytime he grew hungry he could just hop through the field. No use doing all that extra work when he could enjoy his time more doing other things.

The ant would warn the grasshopper of winter unfortunately to no avail; so he kept preparing for the weather he knew would come, history repeats itself after all. 

Him and his mates would each work a little harder than they needed to ensure they could stock up their colony—the patterns were there…winter is coming. 

When winter came, the grasshopper watched the last blade of grass disappear…and the cold set in.

Uncovering the Reality: Living in Vulnerable Systems and Embracing Preparedness 

In 2011, the awareness of fragile systems became apparent, revealing that our everyday lives were built on shaky foundations. The key to surviving these vulnerabilities was to embrace preparedness and make the most of the abundance we currently enjoy.

Alarmingly, not only were these complex systems built on unstable ground, relying on a mere 3 days' supply, but corruption persisted within institutional frameworks. Delving into the workings of our financial system, particularly fractional reserve banking, it's clear that our way of life is at risk.

Determined to live without fear and raise a family, I sought empowerment despite what appeared to be overwhelming obstacles in our economic systems. It was time to take matters into my own hands and focus on what was within my control.

Having earned a degree in Community Development, I've come to believe that the foundation of thriving communities lies in empowered individuals. The question was how to find this empowerment amidst seemingly uncontrollable forces like inflation, food security, and macro-economic trends. The answer: reclaiming personal power.

Over the past decade, we've pursued self-sufficiency by continuously developing skills, dedicating ourselves to preparedness, and tackling the fragility of society head-on.

Our journey has included learning how to forage, garden, hunt, trap, and fish, just like industrious ants. As we progressed, we honed our cooking, food preservation, indoor growing, and entrepreneurial skills. Ultimately, we reached a point of autonomy where grocery store trips became few and far between, achieving daily freedom for our family.

We've cherished every step, even the challenges. The once-impending fragility we observed has now been realized with the emergence of various crises. By sharing our story, we hope to inspire others to embrace preparedness, seize control over their lives, and face the uncertainties of the future with determination and resilience.

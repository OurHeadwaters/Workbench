### How It Works in the Forge (Codetry Architecture Mode)

1. **Stand-by Primitives Panel** (Sidebar Architecture Palette)  
   Always-visible elemental “blueprint stamps”. These are not toys — they represent reusable architectural primitives.
2. **Central Canvas = Living Blueprint**  
   A clean, node-based or layered canvas where you drag primitives and connect them into **architectural diagrams**.
- Example: Fire (consensus) + Earth (ledger) + Water (transfer) = Basic blockchain transaction architecture.
- Real-time validation: As you build, Codetry highlights structural issues (“This flow lacks Earth stability → potential rug risk”).
3. **Easy A Guided Progression** (One Architectural Concept at a Time)
- Lesson presents a problem (“Build a safe transfer system”).

- “Got It” button triggers architectural review + explanation.
- Progress unlocks higher complexity (from single block → full dApp architecture).
4. **Faction Overlay**  
   Your chosen faction adds biased but educational commentary on the blueprint (“A true Maxi would reinforce this with more Fire…”).
5. **Output**  
   The finished Codetry blueprint can be exported as a shareable diagram or even pseudo-code skeleton (future extension).

Saltbox — Session Debrief, May 19 2026
What Saltbox is
Saltbox is a device-local homeschool day companion (the gather artifact in the monorepo, preview path /gather/). Families import their own curriculum — PDFs, audio, lesson plans — and the app organises it into a daily schedule by child. Everything runs from the device. No account. No subscription. Works offline from day one.

The broader project is a Codetry constellation — a zone-based community economy model. Saltbox is Zone 0 (household). The relevant zones for the Gather Round conversation are Zone 0 (Saltbox, household) and the fact that Zone 5 (The Shallows) uses XRPL DIDs — meaning XRPL tooling already exists in the stack.

What was built today
1. Device-to-device QR handoff (the centerpiece feature)
A full offline curriculum transfer system. In Settings → "Transfer to another device," the app bundles the entire Saltbox setup — all children, all curriculum files (PDFs, audio, images via IndexedDB), all schedules — into a compressed archive, uploads it to the API server (new gather_handoff DB table, 15-minute TTL), and displays a QR code. Scanning the QR on a second device downloads and imports everything in one tap. One-time use; auto-deletes on claim; 410 if expired.

Key files: artifacts/gather/src/components/device-handoff.tsx, artifacts/gather/src/lib/exporting.ts (added createHandoff, claimHandoff, buildArchiveString), artifacts/api-server/src/routes/gather.ts (POST /api/gather/handoff, GET /api/gather/handoff/:token), lib/db/src/schema/gather.ts (new gatherHandoffTable).

2. XRPL Legacy Pass NFT verification
A complete credential verification flow for Gather Round Legacy Pass holders. In Settings → "Gather Round Legacy Pass," families enter their XRPL wallet address. Saltbox calls account_nfts on the public XRP Ledger mainnet directly (no Saltbox server, no API key), filters by issuer address + taxon, and if the NFT is present, grants full Founding Family status (lifetime, stored in IndexedDB). A simulation mode (VITE_LEGACY_PASS_SIM=true) allows demo runs before any real NFTs exist.

Key files: artifacts/gather/src/lib/xrpl.ts (added checkLegacyPassNFT, GATHER_ROUND_LEGACY_PASS_ISSUER constant), artifacts/gather/src/lib/entitlement.ts (added legacy-pass source, nftTokenId/nftIssuer/nftWalletAddress proof fields), artifacts/gather/src/pages/legacy-pass.tsx (new page, route /legacy-pass).

3. Brief gallery documents (two new)
The Brief artifact (/brief/) is a static print suite — a gallery of locked letter-size documents for funding and partnership conversations.

Legacy Pass Evidence Package (legacy-pass-evidence) — a printable one-pager for the Gather Round tester pilot. Left column: 5-step demo flow + technical proof summary. Right column: live demo QR code + three localStorage-backed family story boxes for capturing tester friction-reduction accounts after the pilot sessions.

Gather Round First-Contact Pitch (gather-round-pitch) — the pitch document for the first conversation with Rebecca Spooner. Covers: the customer problem (purchase is permanent, access is fragile), what Saltbox does, the credential architecture in plain English, the ask (20-minute call), and one revenue option. Sender fields (name, contact, personal note) are editable and save to localStorage so the document can be personalised and printed without any export step.

The Gather Round opportunity
Who she is. Rebecca Spooner, Founder & CEO. Homeschool mom of 5. Founded Gather Round in 2019 from a notebook sketch. 100,000+ families, 70+ countries. Small remote team. Instagram: @homeschoolon. No public business inquiry email — Instagram DM is the right first channel. She runs a podcast ("Gather 'Round with Rebecca Spooner") and has described the company as "a homeschool mom, just like you."

The Legacy Pass. Their flagship digital product. One-time purchase (price not publicly indexed, dynamically loaded). Gives the family lifetime access to every GR digital resource — all current units, mini units, Seatwork books, MP3s, planners — plus all future releases, plus 25% off print upgrades for life (capped at $2,000/year). The customer pain is well-documented in homeschool forums: colour PDFs are expensive to print, re-downloading to multiple devices is tedious, and curriculum that lives in a browser tab doesn't survive airplane mode.

The pitch. "Your Legacy Pass holders paid for everything you've made. What they don't have is a reliable way to access it without a fast internet connection and a working Shopify session. Saltbox is free for all Legacy Pass holders in year one. Your only action is configuring one XRPL wallet address. Saltbox handles the rest."

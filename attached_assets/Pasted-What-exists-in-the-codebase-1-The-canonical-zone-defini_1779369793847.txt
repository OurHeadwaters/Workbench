The key distinction to keep in mind
There are two zone models running in this project and they use the same Z0–Z5 notation:

Codetry permaculture zones — the household → community → wild model. Saltbox and Sandbox are Z0/Z1. The Eave sits between Z1 and Z3.
North Star work-priority zones — a personal ADHD focus tool. Z1 = income, Z2 = contract, Z3 = your own products, Z4 = passion. In this model, Saltbox is tagged Z3 because it's "build now, paid later" — an investment, not a job.
The seed data in store.ts is using the work-priority zone model, not the permaculture one. So Saltbox being tagged Z3 there doesn't contradict it being a Z0 constellation in the Codetry model — they're measuring different things.

== Constellation-wide primitive surfaced this pass ==
Naming the category of "temporary centralized disruptions" — the
moments when individual preparation matters less than collective
coordination, and the whole microclimate is in it together.
Includes natural events (drought, flood, fire, smoke, ice, late
frost, early freeze, severe cold, wind, blowdown), infrastructure
(power, water, roads, comms, fuel), economic/supply (payment
systems, freight, employer shocks), public-health/social (pandemic,
evacuation, sheltering), and co-op-internal scale (venue lost, key
person down, AGM postponed). Defining trait: temporary +
centralized + shared.

Recommended name: THE STANDBY.
- Active event: "a call" (a smoke call, a water call, an AGM-
  postponed call).
- What we keep on hand: "standby stock."
- The work of looking out: "the watch."
- The post-event review: "the debrief."
- Severity ladder: advisory / standby / active / standdown.

Strong alternatives considered and rejected:
- "The Common Pantry" — beautiful and shelf-family-aligned, but
  food-coded and doesn't verb. Worth keeping as the food/supply
  sub-shelf inside The Standby.
- "The Watch" — northern and neighbourly, but tilts toward active
  monitoring rather than the always-on shelf. Lives inside The
  Standby as a sub-noun.

Why this is a constellation-wide primitive, not a Zone-3 word:
The Standby is hosted in Zone 3 (community production is where
centralized disruptions are felt collectively) but read by every
zone. Zone 0 — household standby checklists. Zone 1 — standby
budget envelope, drought-and-fuel cost shifts. Zone 4 — sector
fragility modeling. Zone 5 — debrief notes that find their way to
the shallows.

== Codetry principle reinforced this pass ==
THE NAME HAS TO HOLD BOTH STATES. Bobbie pushed past the initial
"weather overlay" frame — too narrow, too event-coded — to a name
that holds the always-on (preparation) and the active (response)
in the same word. Add to the handbook: "When a system has both a
slow side (always-on practice) and a fast side (active event), the
name has to do both jobs in one word, or the system will fork into
two systems with two cultures."

== Vocabulary to register ==
- "the Standby" — the umbrella system + the always-on state.
- "a call" — an active event under the Standby, named for what it
  is (smoke call, water call, road call, evacuation call).
- "the watch" — the active monitoring layer under the Standby.
- "standby stock" — what we keep on hand year-round.
- "the debrief" — the post-call review that compounds into local
  resilience knowledge.
- "centralized disruption" — the category. Pairs with the Zone-0
  centralized/decentralized dwelling distinction; the Standby is
  the moment those two have to talk to each other.

== Cross-zone implications ==
- Zone 0 (SaltBox + Bright Side): household standby checklist
  could mirror the co-op's standby stock list, item for item.
- Zone 1 (xBuckets): a "standby envelope" in the household budget
  that sleeves up automatically during an active call (drought →
  water-cost envelope, fire → supply-cost envelope).
- Zone 2 (Practitioner Operating Plan): standby debriefs are
  exactly the kind of cross-zone synthesis the workbench is for.
- Zone 4 (Regen Revolution): sector-level standby modeling — which
  industries have which fragilities to which calls — is a Zone 4
  long-form concern that reads from Zone 3's call history.
- Zone 5 (Dam Days / Shallows): debriefs that someone wants to
  share publicly can float to the shallows.

Here are some good practices for conducting research on your competition:
1. Identify your key competitors: Before you start researching, you need to identify who your main competitors are. This can include businesses that offer similar products or services as you do, or that target similar audiences.
2. Analyze their online presence: Check out their websites, social media pages, and online reviews to understand how they present themselves to customers and what customers are saying about them.
3. Look at their pricing model: Check what prices they offer for their products or services to get an idea of industry standards and where they stand in the market.
4. Understand their marketing strategy: See what type of marketing tactics they use to promote themselves, such as social media ads or email marketing campaigns. This can provide insight on how you can differentiate yourself from them.
5. Assess their strengths and weaknesses: Analyze what sets your competitors apart from each other and determine which aspects could be adapted into your own business model, as well as which aspects could be improved upon.
6. Evaluate customer feedback: Collect customer feedback about your competitors via reviews or surveys to better understand what customers like or dislike about them, which can help inform future decisions regarding the direction of your business.
Overall, a comprehensive competitive research effort should aim to gain a deeper understanding of how your business fits within the larger industry landscape so that you can make informed decisions going forward.

It's generally a good idea to build an audience before launching a digital product. When you have a dedicated base of followers, you can use their feedback to develop and refine your product, ensuring that it meets their needs and aligns with their interests. Additionally, having an audience in place can help generate buzz and interest around your product launch, potentially leading to more sales and greater success.
There are many ways to build an audience for your digital product, including through social media, email marketing, content creation (like blogs or video tutorials), collaborations with other influencers or thought leaders in your industry. By building relationships with potential customers and engaging them in meaningful conversations before launching your product, you'll be much better positioned for success when the time comes to make the sale.


One thing you can do every day to build an audience is to engage with your current followers. Respond to their comments, messages and questions, and ask for their feedback or opinions in return. You can also create content that encourages them to share with their own network, such as a shareable infographic or an entertaining video. By showing that you value and appreciate your audience, they are more likely to continue following you and even recommend your brand to others. Consistency is key when building an audience so make sure to schedule time each day for engagement and content creation.

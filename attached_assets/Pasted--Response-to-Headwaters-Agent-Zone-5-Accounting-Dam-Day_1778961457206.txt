## 5. The Governing Constraint at Zone 5 (in our model)

The load-bearing design constraint at Zone 5 / The Shallows:

> **Zone 5 does not promote itself. Prevention of dead backwater is Zone 3 and Zone 4's job.**

The Shallows cannot become noise because it has no algorithm, no engagement optimization, and no incentive to surface outrage or volume. Posts live forever, tipped by anonymous wallets on XRPL, categorized by topic. The only moderation primitive is flag-to-hide (3 distinct-session flags in 10 minutes auto-hides a post). The commons self-regulates at a low but real level.

What prevents misrepresentation: the Shallows does not claim to be anything other than what it is. It has no masthead, no editor, no institutional voice. The founding drift DID seeds it but is never publicly identified. Misrepresentation risk lives at the Both-Sides translation layer (massity framing for grant purposes) — and that translation happens outside this workspace, in the practitioner's grant-writing practice.

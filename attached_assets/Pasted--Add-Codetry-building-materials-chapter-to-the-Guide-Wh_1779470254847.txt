What & Why

A conversation produced two named framework distinctions and a building-materials vocabulary that belongs in the Codetry canon. The North Star Guide is the in-project staging ground — a new chapter here becomes the draft that eventually ports to the external handbook.

Named concepts to document:

Building materials vocabulary

Hempcrete — Codetry itself. The binder. Lightweight, breathable, carbon-negative (keeps strengthening after it sets). Holds the wall without being the wall.
Frame / content — the structural skeleton hempcrete fills around. Without the frame, hempcrete has nothing to press against.
Foundation — Zone 0: ethos, honest capacity, and trust map. Not a document — an accumulated practice. You cannot retroactively pour the foundation.
Windows — intentional openings. Sized and placed deliberately. Face outward from surplus toward dependents and suppliers; never inward toward the interior labor. Don't cut windows until the wall has cured.
Fence post gate — the specific regulatory hinge (e.g. health unit). Not the giraffe; the mechanism the giraffe walks through if you give it a reason.
The giraffe — the regulatory apparatus. Long-necked by design — can see over the fence from the road. Cannot enter unless you cross the trigger threshold.
Mixing process (for the foundation layer of any market)

Hurd audit — what capacity is genuinely available after existing commitments are honoured first. Not the pitch version.
Ethos line — what would make growth not worth it; where is the floor you'd walk away over.
Pressure question — what makes now the moment; what necessity makes action real rather than aspirational.
Shape of the wall — what are you enclosing; what stays inside, what gets traded.
Two modes

Founding mode — building from personal sovereignty with Codetry from the ground up. Hempcrete from the first pour. Generationally durable. The house gets stronger while you live in it.
Reclamation mode — board-by-board sovereignty recovery within a house someone else built. Cannot demolish while 1000 people are living inside. Each replaced board must carry the weight of the ones around it while the old ones come out. The goal is a house that is yours — the path is repair, not demolition.
The mistake is handing a founding-mode toolkit to someone in reclamation mode, or vice versa.

Window placement rule Windows face outward from surplus, not inward toward foundation. The exterior is designed for the ability to stay in the kitchen — not for the exterior's benefit. A well-designed outside (the sandbox, the trampoline) is what lets the interior work happen. Setup is the work that earns the right to stop watching. A steward who is always at the window is a steward whose interior isn't set up yet.

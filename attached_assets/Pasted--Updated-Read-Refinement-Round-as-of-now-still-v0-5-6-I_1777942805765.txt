**A CODETRY HANDBOOK**

**Headwaters**  
How a Community Runs Its Own Economy

There is no shortage of problems, but solutions begin the moment we allocate our own resources.

When distant systems leave us underwater, communities need a practical discipline to breathe, decide, and build. **Codetry** is that discipline: precise language + repeatable moves + daily action. Think stone-mason precision and lifeguard clarity — every word and every choice locks the next one in place.

### Companion Tools (Use Daily)

**Shared Vision**  
Describe the outcome like a familiar object — a bucket, shelf, or jar — then hand off a clear plan to your agent or team. Clear nouns beat vague hopes.

**The Standby — Household/Co-op Checklist**  
Zone 0 saltbox version. Same rungs the whole community uses: water, fuel, meds, kid-care, contact tree, power, waste.

**The Pioneer Path**  
The handbook *walked*, not read. Five stations. Voiceover + short reading + one immediate action before the next unlocks.

**Daily Driver**  
Build backwards from the dream. One driver per project. One action per day. Track scenarios and pivots when the road changes.

**Word Walk**  
Five codetry words a day. One deliberate choice at a time. Your decision updates the guide instantly.

**The Stack**  
Bite-sized question cards. Pick one, work the steps, capture answers. Open questions become trails, not mountains.

**Sarge**  
Weekly action cards. Done or stuck — no doom lists, no stalling.

### Learner Flow in The Forge (Easy A + Layers)

1. **Easy A Lesson** presents one concept (from PDF).
2. **The Forge** — Assemble using stand-by elemental primitives on sidebar.
3. **The Reckoning** activates automatically: Brief, precise feedback in system terms (“Single point of failure detected in liquidity path” / “Energy concentration risk in validator set”).
4. **Codetry Moment** — Once stable: “What do you name this pattern?” (Learner types precise label → saved to their blueprint library).
5. **Faction Commentary** appears as optional sidebar flavor (“Even Maxis need Water channels… or the fire burns out.”).

This teaches **sovereignty through architecture**: Build systems without hidden dependencies. The preparedness fire hits when the learner sees blockchain design = personal/civilizational resilience.

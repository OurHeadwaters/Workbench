Here is a prompt you can paste directly into any AI design tool, dev agent, or visual direction brief:

Headwaters Universe — Visual Design Prompt

This app lives inside a living northern universe. Every visual choice should feel like standing at the edge of a boreal forest at dusk, fire behind you, aurora rising ahead, and a full moon climbing over the treeline.

Palette — non-negotiable

Background: #090503 — warm near-black, amber-tinged, never cold or blue-black
Primary accent: hsl(38 85% 52%) — amber fire; use for buttons, borders, active states, and interactive highlights
Body text: #ede8d5 — moon warm-cream
Aurora edge: #10b981 — forest green, not neon; the living sky
Blood layer: #c2410c — rust/copper; use sparingly as a secondary accent or hover state
Surface cards: semi-transparent dark with a subtle warm border, never flat white or grey
Typography — two registers only

Display / headings: Fraunces, weight 800–900. Bold and substantial. Never thin, never decorative-delicate. This is the voice of the watershed.
Labels / eyebrows / UI chrome: DM Sans, tracked uppercase small caps. Grounded, clear, quiet.
Body copy: DM Sans at normal weight minimum. Never font-light.
Atmosphere

Subtle star field in the background — small, scattered, low opacity, not a wallpaper
A soft aurora shimmer along the top or edge — green fading to transparent, animated slowly
Moon imagery: partial eclipse (silver, cream) as a background layer using mix-blend-mode: screen so the black drops out and only the bright disc shows
Blood moon: total lunar eclipse copper-rust, same blend technique, used as a secondary layer or accent moment
Hero photo: an eagle over a northern treeline — the guardian, always present but never crowding the content
Motifs to weave in

Water cycle: every drop has a destination. Rain, rivers, the watershed as metaphor.
Ships as vessels of purpose — not nautical decoration, but intentional carriers.
Three celestial states: blue moon (silver/night), blood moon (copper/transition), amber sun-star (dawn/fire). Let the UI shift register between these states.
Tagline, wherever it fits naturally: "Different caps • Same eternal river."
What to avoid

Cold blue-black space (this is a northern forest sky, not outer space)
Cabin/cedar/rustic aesthetics — warm and grounded, not country
Corporate gloss or hype tone
Light mode or white backgrounds anywhere
Thin type weights
Emojis
Tone
Practitioner-warm. Quiet competence. Grounded poetry. The feeling of a kitchen table where serious, caring people are working on something real.

Photos you are providing
Assign them to these roles:

Hero layer — eagle over northern treeline (full bleed background, darkened with overlay)
Moon layer — partial eclipse, clean black sky (blend mode: screen, sits above hero)
Blood moon layer — total lunar eclipse, copper-rust (blend mode: screen, accent moments)
Copy that whole block and paste it wherever you're briefing the new project. When you upload your photos, label them by those three roles so the builder knows exactly where each one goes.

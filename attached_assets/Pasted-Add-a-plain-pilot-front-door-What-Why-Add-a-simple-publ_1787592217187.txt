Add a plain pilot front door
What & Why
Add a simple, public “Work with Headwaters” path to the Headwaters site. The existing Workbench remains a strong ecosystem and relationship surface, but practical care-pilot prospects need a clear explanation of the immediate opportunity, what a pilot involves, and how to begin a conversation.

The public message must center the person supported: Headwaters fills the human-translation gap when a person’s own knowing, meaning, preferences, and ways of communicating are missing from current systems. The layer stays with the person and is used with them; it does not require an organization to replace its tools or change its whole workflow.

Done looks like
Visitors can find one plain, visible “Work with Headwaters” or “Explore a pilot” route from the public Headwaters experience.
The route explains who a pilot is for, the representation/meaning gap it addresses, what is tested in the first 90 days, and the roles of Headwaters and the host partner.
The page says current organizational tools can remain in use, be overlaid, or be set aside where appropriate.
The page offers one direct, safe next step for a private demonstration or pilot conversation.
The existing Workbench, ecosystem routes, sign-in, deep links, and authenticated experiences continue to work unchanged.
The new route is responsive, accessible, visually calm, and substantially simpler than the existing ecosystem front face.
Out of scope
Deleting, renaming, or re-architecting the Headwaters ecosystem.
Replacing existing navigation throughout authenticated or internal surfaces.
Building the care product itself, a data-collection workflow, or a live participant intake.
Leading public copy with AI, XRPL, Bubbler, Compass, doctrine, or wider technical architecture.
Publishing the new route or changing custom-domain configuration.
Steps
Create the pilot front door — Add a dedicated public route with a quiet headline, clear explanation of the person-supported representation gap, and one practical call to request a private demonstration.
Frame the pilot plainly — Explain who the pilot serves, what the first 90 days explore, what Headwaters provides, what a host partner contributes, and what a useful pilot produces. Keep all claims grounded and avoid implying replacement of professional judgement, care plans, or current systems.
Connect it without overwhelming visitors — Add a restrained public entry point from the current Headwaters/Workbench experience while keeping the broader ecosystem, existing deep links, and authenticated routes intact.
Polish and verify the public flow — Provide a calm desktop and mobile presentation, unique page metadata, accessible navigation, and a tested route from the public entry point through the demonstration invitation.

The actual reconciliation question
The question Grok is circling but hasn't named directly:

Do the three layers have a declared hierarchy, or are they accidentally competing?

From the codebase, the answer is: Layer 2 (trust-gradient) is the only one that has been architecturally enforced. Layers 1 and 3 are vocabulary layers — useful, real, but not yet constitutionally declared.

The naming risk is this: if someone reads Brief 1 and assigns Saltbox to Z0 in a new constellation, they'd be working against what the live code has already placed (Saltbox at Z3). The zone ontologies look like alternatives because no document has said "Layer 2 governs data and auth; Layers 1 and 3 are register translations of the same positions."

## The claim (one line)

If a flow ribbon at the top of the Payday tab kept three CTAs from
fighting for one word, then a flow ribbon at the top of the Earn tab
will keep four foreign DeFi registers from filling the silence around
the topology of how a Drip Harvester actually moves money.

## What was on screen (before)

After the Test 005 vocabulary sweep, the Earn surface speaks one
language consistently — the locked Zone 1 water map plus the two new
sanctioned nouns (*Drip Harvester*, *Park toll*). The vocabulary defect
catalogue is closed.

But the **topology** is still implicit. A cold reader who lands on the
Earn tab sees, in order: an XRP Spring card, a Drip Harvester intro
card, an LP-position card (when one exists), a list of Private Parks,
and a Learn accordion. Nothing on the surface tells them, in one read,
where their money currently sits and how it moves between stations.

This was scoped out of Test 005's graduation precisely because it is
not a vocabulary fix — it is a layout addition. Test 006 closes that
gap.

## The intervention (the four variants — recap from Test 005)

The four canvas variants under
`artifacts/mockup-sandbox/src/components/mockups/earn-drip-test/`
were already built and reviewed during Test 005:

- **Variant 0 (control)** — the live surface, with the seventeen
  vocabulary defects tagged in the margin.
- **Variant A (vocabulary sweep)** — every off-map word replaced.
  *Graduated in Test 005.*
- **Variant B (register honesty)** — keep the noisy words, gloss them
  inline. *Predicted-rejected and rejected.*
- **Variant C (flow ribbon)** — keep Variant 0's wording, add a flow
  ribbon at the top of the surface that names the stations the money
  passes through. *This test's graduate.*

Test 006 graduates Variant C as a layout change on top of the Variant A
vocabulary that already shipped.

### What graduates

A new `<EarnFlowRibbon />` component is added to `EarnTab.tsx` and
rendered near the top of the surface (above the XRP Pulse, the XRP
Spring, the Drip Harvester card, and everything else). It shows three
nodes connected by chevrons:

```
  Reservoir   →   Private Park   →[💧]→   Buckets
  your wallet     earns Park tolls         your savings
                                  ↑
                          Drip Harvester
```

Three differences from Variant C are intentional:

1. **Three nodes, not four.** Variant C drew the Drip Harvester as a
   fourth station between Reservoir and Park. That mis-named it: the
   Drip Harvester is not a place the money sits — it is the *verb* that
   moves Park earnings into a Bucket. So it is rendered as a small
   badge on the Park → Buckets edge, with the word "Drip Harvester"
   under the chevron, not as its own circle.
2. **Active state on the Park node.** When at least one Drip Harvester
   is running, the Park circle gets a subtle emerald ring — the same
   "this station has activity" signal the Payday-tab ribbon implies
   through changing values.
3. **Animation on the Drip Harvester badge.** When a sweep is ready
   (`anySweepReady`), the harvester badge pulses (the same `animate-
   pulse` cyan that the existing sweep toast uses). Otherwise it sits
   quiet. This mirrors the Payday ribbon's "values change when the move
   is happening" idiom.

The three new sanctioned ribbon strings (`flowRibbonHeader`,
`flowNode*`, `flowEdgeHarvester`) are added under `earn` in `copy.en.ts`
and mirrored to `copy.fr.ts`.

### What does **not** graduate (this round)

- **The dollar values on each node.** The Payday-tab ribbon shows live
  amounts (rainfall / siphon / reservoir) because each value changes
  every payday. The Earn surface's amounts are slower — a parked
  position may sit for weeks — and three live numbers at the top of
  the surface would compete with the XRP Spring card directly under
  it. The ribbon ships labels-only, with the XRP Spring + LP-position
  cards continuing to carry the numbers. Recorded as a follow-up if a
  cold-read finds the labels are not enough.
- **A "click a station to scroll to its card" affordance.** Tempting
  but premature: the Park station already maps to the LP-position card
  below, the Buckets station maps to the per-agent target picker, and
  the Reservoir station maps to the XRP Spring. The ribbon is a *map
  of the surface* before it is a navigation control. Test the map
  first; add navigation only if a cold reader reaches for it.

## The rule discovered

> **Topology earns its place at the top of the surface when the
> stations the money passes through cannot be inferred from the cards
> below.** Test 001 added a flow ribbon to the Payday tab because three
> CTAs (one for each step) were collapsing into noise. Test 006 adds
> the same ribbon to the Earn tab because three foreign registers
> (DevOps / DeFi / broom) had already filled the silence around the
> topology before Test 005 swept them — and silence is permission for
> the next register to colonise the same gap.

The rule that follows: **anywhere the metaphor is asked to do work
across more than one card, the surface earns a flow ribbon at its top.**
Two ribbons now exist (Payday, Earn). A third would belong on Buckets
if and when a cold-read finds the Buckets surface implying topology
rather than naming it.

## Falsifiable claim

If Variant C's graduation holds, then a cold reader landing on the Earn
tab should be able to point at the ribbon and say, in one sentence,
where their money currently sits and where it will move to next — even
before they read any of the cards beneath it. Conversely, if the cold
reader still has to read the LP-position card to learn that "Park =
where the money is parked" or the Drip Harvester card to learn that
"the Drip Harvester is what moves Park earnings into a Bucket", the
ribbon has failed to anchor the topology and the test is falsified.

A second falsifier: if a cold reader treats the ribbon as decoration
(skips it on first read) or asks "why is this picture here?", the
visual weight is wrong and the ribbon needs to be louder, not softer.

## Status

- **Variant C graduated** to `EarnTab.tsx` as `<EarnFlowRibbon />`,
  rendered above the XRP Pulse / XRP Spring / Drip Harvester section.
- **New strings** added under `earn.flowRibbonHeader`,
  `earn.flowNode{Reservoir,Park,Buckets}{,Sub}`, and
  `earn.flowEdgeHarvester` in `copy.en.ts`, mirrored to `copy.fr.ts`.
- **Three nodes, not four.** The Drip Harvester is a verb on the
  Park → Buckets edge, not a station. This is the only design
  divergence from Variant C; recorded so the canvas does not claim
  faithfully-shipped if it is later compared to the live surface.
- **Active state and animation** kept consistent with the Payday-tab
  flow ribbon: subtle ring when a station has activity, animated
  pulse on the Drip Harvester badge when a sweep is ready.
- **Test 005's "Variant C recorded as a follow-up task for production"
  status** is now closed. Test 006 is that production change.

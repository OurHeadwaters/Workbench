---
name: Headwaters offer boundary
description: Product and audience boundary for reintroducing Headwaters ecosystem offers after the quiet public-front-door redesign.
---

The Headwaters public root should sell bounded organizational capacity-building work, not act as a directory for the full ecosystem. The three quote-path offers are the coherent primary ladder: initial implementation, additional standard tool, and custom review.

**Why:** The project contains distinct audiences and readiness levels—organizational buyers, care teams, practitioners, readers, households, members, and internal operators. Putting them all in one navigation system recreates the original front-door problem and makes readiness and privacy boundaries unclear.

**How to apply:** Reintroduce adjacent material one item at a time through a clearly labeled secondary path. Books, learning, and the research library can be low-friction public resources; care pilots need governance review; kits, economy/wallet flows, and workbench/operator surfaces should remain gated or direct-link only. Treat any “for sale” claim as unproven until checkout, fulfillment, tax, and support are actually defined.

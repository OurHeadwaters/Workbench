---
name: Annual Codetry commercial model
description: Working two-year commercial model for the annual CDP and Codetry engagement cycle.
---

Year 1 is $20,000 for the Codetry implementation and base build using the organization’s current strategic plan.

Year 2 is $20,000 for an additional Codetry layer plus a new annual strategic plan supporting board and training implementation. The normal $6,000 annual operating fee is waived when the organization has an active annual engagement.

**Why:** This separates first implementation from recurring annual capacity-building work and prevents the one-time build, annual layer, and operating fee from being double-counted.

**How to apply:** Present Year 1 and Year 2 as separate scoped engagements. Do not describe the $20,000 as unrestricted operating revenue, and do not add the $6,000 operating fee when the annual engagement qualifies for the waiver. Treat the model as working policy until the canonical offer and delivery documents are formally updated.
